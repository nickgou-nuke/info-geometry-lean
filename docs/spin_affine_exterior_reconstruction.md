## 1. Spin, affine coordinates, and the actual inverse metric

```

E = M^{-1},
E (S' - b I) = S.
```

Invertibility is represented by a genuine matrix unit, not an inverse identity assumed as a theorem input. An additional anisotropic B after this inverse gives B S, not S.

There are two different Gram matrices:

```
sum_i (sum_j M_ij S_j)^2 = sum_jk (M^T M)_jk S_j S_k,
C_original = sum_pq (E^T E)_pq (S'_p-b_p I)(S'_q-b_q I).
```

All operator products retain their order. For invertible M, `E^T E=(M M^T)^{-1}`. The exact inverse-frame identity is `E (M M^T) E^T=I`. The stream's `E G^{-1} E^T=I` with `G=M^T M` is not this identity.

The correct transformed spin bracket, derived from the cofactor identity, is

```

```

Here the cofactor index is `(M^{-1})_{mp}`, not `(M^{-1})_{pm}` in the first contraction. The new Lean general bracket theorem retains the original double sum without presupposing a Levi-Civita tensor convention. The output-coordinate cofactor formula above is a mathematical reconstruction, not a separately declared Lean theorem in this extension. A nonnormal shear regression distinguishes the two Gram matrices exactly.

Central offsets disappear from the raw commutator. Their reappearance as constants in a shifted basis is removable by centering. This is not a nontrivial central extension or a quantum anomaly. The affine displacement is not a linear Lie automorphism and hence is not an outer Lie automorphism. The source includes a direct trace obstruction to ANY matrix similarity producing a nonzero scalar shift.

The inhomogeneous quadric in S' coordinates is centered at +b. Its classical ellipsoid semi-axes are the original radius times the singular values of M, not the eigenvalues of an inverse Gram matrix. A single affine ellipsoid does not establish a confocal family. Noncommuting spin components do not possess a joint classical sphere spectrum.

## 2. Which Bogoliubov-shaped transformations preserve spin?

For the existing spin-half matrices define

```
P = u S_+ + v S_-,
Q = conjugate(v) S_+ + conjugate(u) S_-.
```

The exact calculations are

```
[P,Q] = 2 (|u|^2-|v|^2) S_z,
[S_z,P] - P = -2v S_-.
```

Therefore the full fixed-Cartan relations in this ansatz require `v=0` and `|u|^2=1`. The hyperbolic norm alone tests just the first equation. The rational pair `(u,v)=(5/4,3/4)` is an explicit counterexample to the claimed full-spin invariance.

The transverse anisotropic transformation has an ordinary inverse:

```
p = ((a+b)/2) S_+ + ((a-b)/2) S_-,
m = ((a-b)/2) S_+ + ((a+b)/2) S_-,
S_+ = ((a+b)/(2ab)) p - ((a-b)/(2ab)) m,  a b != 0.
```

This is not an extra squeeze after already applying M^{-1}. Positive real a,b are needed to introduce a real squeeze parameter and real square roots. The stream's forward/inverse sign and ratio conventions for r are mixed.

Spin lowering is a linear operator, even though it is the adjoint of raising. S_y is Hermitian; i S_y is anti-Hermitian. A hyperbolic transformation of bosonic CCR and a transformation of finite-spin SU(2) generators are different operations. The unitary spin operator generated by S_+^2-S_-^2 does not generally act by the claimed linear bosonic mixing. In spin one-half its generator is identically zero, as the source proves.

The harmonic wobbling Hamiltonian requires a specified Hamiltonian, a stable expansion axis, a large-spin regime, and control of the approximation. No exact wobbling spectrum, remainder estimate, or bosonization theorem is inferred here.

## 3. Casimir shifts and weight-projector qualifications

The polynomial identities

```

(-s-1)(-s)=s(s+1)
```

and the actual spin-half ladder Casimir are included. These identities are not the Harish-Chandra isomorphism or a proof that the universal-enveloping center is polynomial in one Casimir.

For the usual complexified sl2 discussion, rho=1/2 is the S_z coordinate convention; in the normalization H=2S_z the highest-weight coordinate and rho are doubled. The unshifted Harish-Chandra projection is invariant for the dot action; after the rho shift the ordinary Weyl invariants appear. The dot action here is an affine realization of a finite Weyl group, not the full affine Weyl group.

A ladder shifts an eigenweight only when its image vector is nonzero. It is not an invertible translation of a finite spin spectrum. The Lagrange formula for a spectral projector is correct for distinct eigenvalues of a diagonalizable S_z, but only the two-eigenvalue case yields an affine polynomial. In an S_z eigenstate, the transverse second moment is s(s+1)-m^2 and the transverse means vanish, so it is also the sum of those variances. The value m=0 exists only for integer spin; the smallest |m| for half-integer spin is 1/2. These general representation-theoretic and spectral claims remain outside the formal declarations added here.

## 4. Genuine exterior CAR and the Zorn regular representation

The canonical real Zorn carrier and product are reused, with upper U_i, lower V_i, and diagonal poles u_+,u_-. Native multiplication gives

```
U_i V_j + V_j U_i = delta_ij 1,
U_i U_j + U_j U_i = 0,
V_i V_j + V_j V_i = 0.
```

These raw quadratic identities do not make the octonion product associative. Nevertheless polarized alternativity gives the EXACT operator identity

```
L_a L_b + L_b L_a = L_{ab+ba},
L_a^2=L_{a^2}.
```

Consequently `create_i=L_{U_i}` and `annihilate_i=L_{V_i}` satisfy all three CAR families inside the associative endomorphism algebra. This is a positive result, not a claim that the CAR is merely an analogy. The old circular operator conjugation transports those identities automatically.

Crucially, `L_{ab}=L_a L_b` fails in general. The source evaluates it on `(a,b,x)=(U_0,U_1,U_2)`, where the two native bracketings give u_- and u_+.

With U designated creation, the vacuum VECTOR is u_-:

```

L_{U_i} u_- = U_i,
L_{U_i} u_+ = 0.
```

Reversing creation/annihilation reverses these labels; the stream cannot retain both its stated creation convention and its claimed u_+ vacuum. The actual vacuum projector is


Its range is formally shown to be exactly the span of u_-, and it is idempotent. It is not L_{u_-}, whose range is an entire Peirce half (the independent matrix regression gives ranks 1 and 4).

## 6. Negative Clifford reflection and quaternion/time-reversal distinctions

```

```

The map is lifted to a native algebra homomorphism from `CliffordAlgebra negativeQuadratic`. A unit normal n gives a genuine operator unit with inverse -gamma(n), and

```

```

Each gamma is odd for the existing exterior grade involution; its two-vector products are even. An orthogonal pair of unit normals gives a bivector with square -1. ## 8. Claims deliberately not promoted to theorems

A Bloch map arising as a CPTP channel requires positivity/completely positive constraints. A general GL3 affine operator-frame change is not automatically a channel. In Heisenberg spin-half normalization the scalar offset is half the Bloch translation, not the full translation.

A contraction keeps the vector-space dimension. Three su2 generators cannot contract into the six-dimensional iso3 algebra. Uniform rescaling contracts to an abelian three-space; the familiar six-dimensional Euclidean contraction instead starts with two independent su2 copies. The formula P=S+b/epsilon I in the stream has commutator [P,P]=[S,S], not epsilon^2[S,S].

A scalar central connection lies in an additional abelian factor; it is not one of the three independent iso3 translations. A globally exact abelian form B=db has dB=0 and zero closed-loop integral. Nontrivial holonomy requires additional global data. A full unitary-frame Maurer-Cartan form is flat; curvature of a projected Berry connection is a separate construction. A deformation parameter named gamma does not by itself force a monopole.

No Stiefel-Whitney calculation, Pin bundle existence, global Cartan torsion solution, confocal foliation, quantization of an arbitrary manifold, nuclear wobbling asymptotics, or QCD color interpretation is proved by this extension. Those require their actual carriers and hypotheses; none is inserted to make the local algebra pass.
