# Confabulation Refinement Protocol

## Intent
Use confabulation as a controlled hypothesis generator, not as a truth oracle.

## Five-Stage Loop
1. Excite
- Generate high-novelty candidate hypotheses in `creative_notes`.
- Mark all speculative fragments with `inference_flags`.

2. Contrast
- Seek contradictory frames and adversarial counter-claims.
- Keep both surviving and rejected branches for audit memory.

3. Ground
- Attach source-backed evidence in `literature_evidence`.
- Distinguish direct citation from inferred synthesis.

4. Translate
- Map claims to repo-native owner files, symbols, and target theorems.
- Reject claims that cannot be mapped to owner surfaces.

5. Gate
- Run targeted build and axiom checks.
- Canonical lane admits only claims that survive proof/owner constraints.

## Non-Negotiables
- No canonical edits from raw confabulation output.
- No deletion of rejected high-novelty claims; archive with reason.
- Every accepted claim must be traceable to declaration + evidence + verification.

## Minimal Data Contract
Per segment:
- `creative_notes`: exploratory synthesis
- `claims`: distilled testable statements
- `inference_flags`: explicit uncertainty labels
- `literature_evidence`: URLs and summaries
- `confidence`: calibrated after verification

## Failure Modes
- Symbolic inflation: poetic language with no owner mapping.
- Evidence collapse: claims without source traceability.
- Gate bypass: moving speculative text into canonical files.

## Success Criterion
High-entropy ideation is preserved in research lanes,
while canonical theorem surfaces remain proof-auditable and non-vacuous.
