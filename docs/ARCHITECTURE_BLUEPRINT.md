## Section 1: Self-Dual Cones and Fenchel-Legendre Duality

**Mathematical content:** A cone `K` in a real Hilbert space satisfying `K = K*` (self-duality). The Fenchel-Legendre transform `f*(y) = sup_x(⟨x,y⟩ - f(x))` satisfies `f** = f` for convex `f`.
