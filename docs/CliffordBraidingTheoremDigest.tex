\section{Scope}
This note records a finite central-core packet inspired by Clifford-braiding
language.  It does not prove a general Clifford Braiding Theorem, Lorentz spin
representation theorem, anyon construction, or superKähler/metriplectic
emergence theorem.

\section{Exact finite packet}
Let
\[
J=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
E=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},\qquad I=I_2.
\]
The verified identities are
\[
J^2=-I,
\qquad E^2=I,
\qquad EJ+JE=0.
\]
The split pseudoscalar $P=EJ$ satisfies
\[
P^2=I.
\]
Moreover, since $J^{-1}=-J$,
\[
J I J^{-1}=I,
\qquad
J(-I)J^{-1}=-I.
\]
Thus conjugation by the concrete root $J$ preserves the central sign core
$\{I,-I\}$.

\section{Finite parafermion phase shadow}
For $\omega$ satisfying $\omega^2+\omega+1=0$, the exact clock and shift
matrices
\[
X=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\0&0&1\\1&0&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Z=\mathrm{diag}(1,\omega,\omega^2)
\]
satisfy
\[
X^3=Z^3=I,
\qquad XZ=\omega ZX.
\]
This is a finite $\mathbb Z_3$ phase-grading witness, not a theorem that a
physical parafermion CFT or anyon model has been constructed.
