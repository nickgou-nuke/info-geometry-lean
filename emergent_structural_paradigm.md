## I. Core Mandate: Goutev’s Principle of Absolute Relativity of Measurement

The repository treats measurement as relational rather than absolute.

Goutev’s Principle is a **repository policy for formalization direction**: measurements are relational, normalization is gauge fixing, and physical state-space should first be projectivized before a probability/thermal/metric section is chosen. It is **not** a mathematical theorem already proved by Lean.

There is no primitive isolated measurement. A measurement is a comparison between a state/readout and a reference state, scale, gauge, or observer frame.

### Consequences:

1.  **Projective state first.**
    Raw counts, scores, amplitudes, or weights are first treated as rays or relative profiles.
2.  **Normalization is gauge fixing.**
    Probability, density matrices, thermal states, and entropy readouts appear only after choosing a section through a projective ray.
3.  **Relative potentials over absolute Hamiltonians.**
    The preferred primitive is a relative potential, cocycle, modular generator, or Radon–Nikodym/log-density comparison, not an isolated scalar energy.
4.  **Cocycles over absolute magnitudes.**
    The repo privileges compositional laws: `state/reference`, `source/target`, `before/after`, `algebra/commutant`, `raw/normalized`.
5.  **Scale invariance before thermodynamics.**
    Extensive raw data is not physically final. Intensive or thermodynamic readouts are produced only after Weyl/projective gauge selection.
6.  **Graph evidence is navigation, not proof.**
    Retrieval, DAG connectedness, InfoTree context, and Arango overlays may guide proof repair, but only Lean owner theorems or explicit certificates promote claims.
