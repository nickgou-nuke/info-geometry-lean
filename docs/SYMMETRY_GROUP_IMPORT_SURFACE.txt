Method
------
Starting at `InfoGeometry` and `InfoGeometry.All`, recursively follow repo-owned
Lean `import` and `public import` lines. Compare the resulting module names
with the 339 owner files in `SYMMETRY_GROUP_CARRIER_DECLARATIONS.txt` (562
candidate declarations). The focused index includes group carriers, actions,
representations, and Lie subalgebras; being outside an umbrella is an import
observation, not proof that a module should be imported or defines a group.

External Coxeter pin verification
---------------------------------
The `external_refs/lean-coxeter` submodule is at commit
`857527a5ee35ddb64127654b821aabd99d2c178d`, after tag `v4.35.0`. Its checked-in
`lean-toolchain` is `v4.34.1` and its manifest pins Mathlib
`d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612`, matching the root. The root
`lakefile.lean` exposes the external source through the `Coxeter` library, and
the repo-owned `CoxeterPort` modules lift its geometric and signed-reflection
representations to endomorphism-algebra units. This keeps the external
reference available without changing the root Mathlib pin. The tracked Lean
source count in `SYMMETRY_GROUP_INVENTORY.md` was refreshed to 26,149 on
2026-10-30.
