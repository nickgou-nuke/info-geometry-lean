diracAdjoint, with Ring A, StarRing A, Algebra complex A, StarModule complex A.

imply d#=B and B#=d. Reuse ReflexiveMatrixDirac.pairMatrixLinear_dirac for the
actual native pair and the existing split source metric. These metric hypotheses
