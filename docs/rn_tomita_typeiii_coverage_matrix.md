## Scope and Bounded Search Footprint

This matrix maps the live codebase for Radon–Nikodym, Tomita–Takesaki modular flows, GNS constructions, Type III continuous-core interfaces, and finite/colimit polar realizations into:

- `implemented` — constructive definitions and kernel-checked theorems on the stated carrier
- `interface` — compiled abstract/conditional signature packaging hypotheses rather than constructing the analytic theorem internally
- `shadow` — minimal scalar, combinatorial, Boolean, or fixed-matrix stubs that must not be cited as operator-algebraic Tomita theorems
- `missing` — not yet formalized as an owner theorem surface (indexed with stable frontier IDs `F1`–`F5`)

Bounded search footprint for the `2026-11-26` audit:

- Exhaustive path enumeration of `137` `*tomita*`/`*takesaki*` Lean files across `23` directories, `16` `FilteredGNSTomita*` files, `19` `RelativeModular*` files, and `InfoGeometry/Modular/`.
