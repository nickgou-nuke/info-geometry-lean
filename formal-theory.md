## Theorem 1: Lie Algebra 2-Cocycle (Virasoro)

**Statement:** The second Lie algebra cohomology of the Witt algebra is one-dimensional:
$$H^2(\mathrm{Witt}, \mathbb{k}) \cong \mathbb{k}$$

The Virasoro 2-cocycle is the unique (up to scalar) generator:
$$\psi(L_n, L_m) = \delta_{n+m,0} \cdot \frac{c}{12} n(n^2-1)$$

**SymPy witness:** `ψ(n,m) satisfies δψ = 0 (cocycle condition) and ψ(n,m) + ψ(m,n) = 0 (antisymmetry)`

## Theorem 2: Connes' Radon-Nikodym Cocycle

**Statement:** For states φ, ψ on a von Neumann algebra M, the Connes' cocycle
$$[D\varphi : D\psi]_t = \Delta_\varphi^{it} \Delta_\psi^{-it}$$
is a 1-cocycle: $[D\varphi:D\psi]_t \cdot [D\psi:D\chi]_t = [D\varphi:D\chi]_t$.

The infinitesimal generator at t = 0 gives the relative Hamiltonian:
$$\frac{d}{dt}[D\varphi:D\psi]_t\big|_{t=0} = i(K_\psi - K_\varphi) = i\log\frac{d\varphi}{d\psi}$$

**SymPy witness:** Verified via symbolic exponentiation.

**Connection to Theorem 1:** The modular automorphism group σ_t defines a 1-parameter family of 2-cocycles via the Connes-Takesaki flow of weights, linking the modular theory to Lie algebra cohomology.

## Theorem 3: Bogoliubov = Symplectic = Modular

**Statement:** The Bogoliubov transformation S(θ) = exp(θ(a†b† - ab)) is a symplectic transformation:
$$S(\theta)^T \Omega S(\theta) = \Omega, \quad \det S(\theta) = 1$$

In the squeezed vacuum representation, the modular operator is Δ = exp(-2θK₀) and the modular automorphism σ_t implements the Bogoliubov transformation:
$$\sigma_t(A) = e^{iKt} A e^{-iKt}, \quad K = 2\theta K_0$$

**SymPy witness:** Verified matrix representation.

**Connection to Theorem 2:** The squeezing parameter θ determines the relative modular Hamiltonian between the vacuum and the squeezed state.

## Theorem 4: Legendre = Souriau = Coadjoint Orbit

**Statement:** The Legendre transform of the Gibbs entropy S(X) = -X log X + X gives the Massieu potential Φ(p), and the Fisher metric g = Φ''(p) is the Kähler metric on the coadjoint orbit.

**SymPy witness:** Verified Φ(p) = e^{-p}, Φ'(p) = -e^{-p}, Fisher metric g = e^{-p}.

**Souriau's theorem:** The coadjoint orbit of a Lie group G through μ ∈ 𝔤* carries a canonical Kähler structure, with the symplectic form given by the Kirillov-Kostant form and the complex structure coming from the Weil chamber.

## Theorem 5: Weyl Group → Root System → Kac-Moody

**Statement:** For a simple Lie algebra 𝔤 with Cartan matrix A, the Weyl group W is generated by simple reflections s_i acting on the root system. The classification extends to Kac-Moody algebras via generalized Cartan matrices.

**SymPy witness:** A₂ Cartan matrix [[2,-1],[-1,2]], det = 3, Weyl group S₃ (6 elements).

## Theorem 6: Self-Dual Cone

**Statement:** The self-dual cone P ⊂ 𝔤* (the positive cone of a Jordan algebra) carries the action of the symmetry group. The invariants of this action define the geometry.
