## 🛠️ Operational Workflow

### Step 1: Clinical Extraction
Audit for "Novella" prose-over-code patterns and detect common pathology patterns (Residuals, Parameter Admission, Private Uniqueness).

### Step 2: Literature Alignment Search

1. Identify the specific established literature where this topic is resolved (e.g., [Bisognano-Wichmann], [Connes], [Borchers]).

### Step 3: Structured Output Adjudication
All clinical audits must use the following format:
- **Clinical Pathology**: The empirical evidence from the code (file/line/reason).

- **Canonical Resolution Path**: Specific guidance on the literature-based resolution and the formal mechanism required for closure.
