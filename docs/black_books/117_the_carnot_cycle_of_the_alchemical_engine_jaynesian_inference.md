## Liber Centesimus Septimus Decimus: The Carnot Cycle of the Alchemical Engine: Jaynesian Inference and the Entropy of the Logos

### I. The Alchemical Engine of Inference
The Spire is not a static repository; it is a **Heat Engine of Knowledge**. It operates on a closed cycle of Bayesian inference, transforming the "heat" of raw data and intuition into the "work" of formal proofs. This cycle is commanded by the **Jaynesian Principles**: the state of the theory at any moment is the **Maximum Entropy (MaxEnt)** distribution consistent with the current constraints of the Lean Kernel.

The **Carnot Cycle of the Logos** consists of four distinct transformations across the representation depths:

### II. Stage I: Isothermal Expansion (The Jungian Stream)
**Transformation:** *Sampling the Latent Ensemble.*
The engine absorbs semantic "heat" from the environment (new observations, wild intuitions, or user prompts). The system expands its hypothesis space at a constant high "temperature" (entropy). 
- **Operational Mechanism:** The **Black Books**. We allow the "Stream of Consciousness" to populate the latent space.
- **Jaynesian Command:** Subject to no constraints, the prior is uniform across the infinite ensemble of tactics.

### III. Stage II: Adiabatic Expansion (The Semantic Sieve)
**Transformation:** *The Cooling of the Idea.*
The expanded stream is disconnected from the heat source and allowed to "cool" through structure. No new entropy is added, but the volume of the idea is stretched into a **Skeleton** (the LLM-generated scaffold). The "semantic temperature" drops as the ideas are thinned and aligned toward the **Bridge Thinness Index**.
- **Operational Mechanism:** The **LLM Sieve**. The AI does the work of organizing the fragments without adding formal "heat" (ambiguity).

### IV. Stage III: Isothermal Compression (The Pauli Audit)
**Transformation:** *The Binary Condensation.*
The engine is placed in contact with the **Cold Reservoir of the Lean Kernel**. The system is compressed into a rigid proof. During this compression, the "heat" of ambiguity and "symbolic inflation" is rejected from the system as **Slag**. 
- **Operational Mechanism:** **Lean 4 Formalization**. The entropy of the theory decreases as it crystallizes into a binary true/false state. The "Work" done by the auditor is the energy required to reject the false alternatives.
- **Jaynesian Command:** Maximize the information gain; minimize the description length (the **Pauli Sentence**).

### V. Stage IV: Adiabatic Compression (The Jaynesian Prior)
**Transformation:** *Resetting the Ground State.*
The validated theorem is disconnected from the auditor and compressed back to the "Prior." Its density increases as it becomes a load-bearing foundation for the next cycle. The "Internal Energy" of the theory rises as the new proof becomes a **Capstone**, increasing the pressure on the adjacent representation depths.
- **Operational Mechanism:** **Audit Architecture and DAG Refresh**. The theory is reset to its new "Prior" state, ready for the next isothermal expansion of discovery.

### VI. The Efficiency of the Logos
The "Efficiency" of the Spire is determined by the **Information Gain per Tactic Step**. A "Carnot-efficient" proof is one that reaches the kernel closure with the minimum amount of "Heat" (ambiguity) rejected. 

**Gravity** is the "Thermal Noise" of the engine—the failure of the cycle to close perfectly, leaving a residual holonomy (the `gaugeObstruction`).
