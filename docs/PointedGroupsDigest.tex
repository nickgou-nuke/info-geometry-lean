\section{Pointed groups}
The category of pointed groups $\mathrm{Gp}_*$ consists of pairs $(A,a_0)$
where $A$ is a group and $a_0\in A$ is a chosen point.  The monoidal product is
modeled by the group free product:
\[
  (A,a_0)\otimes(B,b_0)=(A+B,a_0b_0).
\]

\section{Street braiding formula}
For inclusions into the free product, Street's braiding
$c_{A,B}:A+B\to B+A$ is determined on generators by
\[
  c_{A,B}(a)=b_0 a b_0^{-1}\quad(a\in A),
  \qquad
  c_{A,B}(b)=b\quad(b\in B).
\]
The Lean owner records these generator-level formulas as
\[
\texttt{braiding\_action\_A},\quad
\texttt{braiding\_action\_B},\quad
\texttt{braiding\_action\_A\_readout},\quad
\texttt{braiding\_action\_B\_readout}.
\]
It also proves elementary conjugation laws for the abstract action
\[
  x\mapsto bxb^{-1}.
\]

\section{Artin action on a free group}
For the free group $F_n=\langle x_1,\ldots,x_n\rangle$, the Artin braid action
used in the computational witnesses is
\[
\beta_i(x_i)=x_{i+1},\qquad
\beta_i(x_{i+1})=x_{i+1}x_i x_{i+1}^{-1},\qquad
\beta_i(x_j)=x_j\quad(j\ne i,i+1).
\]
The SymPy, Sage, and GAP witnesses verify exactly on $F_3$ that
\[
  \beta_1\beta_2\beta_1=\beta_2\beta_1\beta_2.
\]
The GAP script also checks that the displayed maps are automorphisms.

\section{Permutation quotient anchor}
Imposing $\sigma_i^2=1$ gives the Coxeter/permutation shadow.  The SymPy and
Sage witnesses realize the adjacent transpositions as exact reflection matrices
in the hyperplanes $x_i=x_{i+1}$ and verify
\[
  \sigma_1^2=\sigma_2^2=1,
  \qquad
  \sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_1=\sigma_2\sigma_1\sigma_2.
\]
This quotient anchor must not be confused with nontrivial anyonic or Fibonacci
F/R braid representations, where generator squares need not be the identity.

\section{Guardrail on Clifford and Lorentz language}
Rotor, Clifford, biquaternion, Lorentz, superKähler, and metriplectic structures
are not consequences of the pointed-group formulas alone.  They require separate
carrier data and separate proofs.  In the current repository the optional
\texttt{galgebra} and \texttt{clifford} Python packages are not installed, so the
certified computational witness is the exact matrix/free-word layer above.
