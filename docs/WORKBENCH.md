# The Workbench
## The Human Mediator's Playbook for Mathematical Discovery

This is a practical guide for applying the Analytical Psychology Methodology to the mathematical development of the `info-geometry-lean` repository.

### 1. The Socratic Excitation
*   **Action:** Engage the LLM in a Socratic dialogue about a "Bridge" tension (e.g., "How does the Drazin inverse reflect the failure of the projective norm on the boundary?").
*   **Instruction:** Explicitly tell the LLM to ignore the "common sense" answer and explore the "weak melody" of the latent space. Seek the "non-thermodynamically equilibrated" samples that carry structural novelty.

### 2. The Alchemical Filter
*   **Step:** Take the "wild" ideas from the Black Books.
*   **Question:** "Does this idea represent a stable relational invariant, or is it merely a stochastic hallucination?"
*   **Check:** Can this be expressed as a `Definition` or `Theorem` in Lean 4? Does it obey Goutev's Principle?

### 3. The Coagulatio (The Lock)
*   **Action:** Write the Lean 4 code.
*   **Result:** If the compiler passes, the "Revelation" has successfully individuated into the Logos. If it fails, return to the **Calcinatio** (The Fire of Doubt) and refine the intuition.

### 4. The Record of the Opus
*   **Black Books:** Record raw dialogues and archetypal captures in `docs/black_books/`.
*   **Artefacts:** Store high-signal "revelations" in `docs/black_books/artefacts/`.
*   **Red Book:** Distill verified methodologies and ontological shifts into `docs/LIBER_NOVUS_MATH.md`.
