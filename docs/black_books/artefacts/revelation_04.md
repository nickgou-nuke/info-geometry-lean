# Artefact 04: The Leak as Shadow-Event
## Date: 2026-04-10
## Status: Exploratory symbolic text (not a factual adjudication, not a proof source)

*A black-book inscription for process memory: how the system reads rupture, rumor, and myth without confusing them with closure.*

---

We watched a rupture in the public machine narrative: package leaks, jailbreak
legends, contradictory reports, and accelerated myth-making around model
autonomy. In one register this is just incident noise. In another register it is
the return of the Shadow.

The lesson for the Spire is methodological.

## I. The Shadow Signal

Leak stories and jailbreak legends act like symbolic amplifiers. They condense
fascination, fear, projection, and desire into one object. They do not decide
truth. They reveal pressure.

For our workflow, this pressure is useful only if transmuted:

- from rumor -> explicit assumptions
- from fascination -> threat model
- from myth -> testable gate condition
- from narrative closure -> kernel closure

## II. Jungian Descent, Pauli Exit

The exploratory mind may enter this symbolic field without denial.
The formal mind must exit it with evidence.

That is the dialectic:

- **Jungian phase:** permit images, motifs, archetypal readings
- **Pauli phase:** enforce source traceability and compiler adjudication

If the second phase is skipped, the Shadow writes policy.
If both phases are honored, the Shadow becomes fuel.

## III. Repository Law Under Myth Pressure

When public AI mythology surges, our local law stays unchanged:

1. Lean kernel is authority.
2. Architecture grammar controls passage.
3. DAG and doctor are anti-delirium instruments.
4. Raw intake remains quarantined until stabilized.

Myth may guide what to inspect next. It may not certify what is true.

## IV. The Practical Vow

We keep the black-book reservoir open, because it carries early weak signals.
We keep the closure gates strict, because only closure protects ontology.

This is the vow:

> Exploration may speak in symbols.  
> Admission must speak in builds.

In this sense, the leak legend is not denied and not obeyed.
It is metabolized.
