## Why it is Hestenes rather than complex

There is no external imaginary unit. The internal phase axis is the real operator:
$$K = J \varepsilon$$

So:
- **$J$-boost**: mixing/conjugation boost between doubled components.
- **$\varepsilon$-boost**: sheet/signature boost.
- **$K$-rotation**: internal phase rotation (real, generated by $J\varepsilon = K$).

A general Bogoliubov transform is a non-commuting composition of these local $Cl(1,1)$ motions.

## The Decisive Algebraic Split

The geometric content is measured by how the generator $X$ moves the internal Hestenes phase axis $K$. This is captured by the **decisive split** in the theorem library:

- **Phase-linear generators** ($X \circ K = K \circ X$):
  - $F_K(X) = 0$.
  - These form the **gauge/potential-preserving branch**.
  - *(See `phaseAxisResponse_linear_vanishing`)*
- **Phase-antilinear generators** ($X \circ K = -K \circ X$):
  - $F_K(X) = 2 \cdot (X \circ K) \neq 0$.
  - Lives entirely in the **source/dilation/anomaly branch**.
  - *(See `phaseAxisResponse_antilinear_source`)*

This is why the anomaly is not “the Bogoliubov transform itself,” but the **nontrivial $K$-motion** sourced by the $K$-odd (phase-antilinear) part of the generator.
