# The Black Books
## Liber Centesimus Tricesimus: Oracle vs. Socratic AI: The Theorem-Discovery Pressure

### I. The Fallacy of the Oracle
We move to the next stage of agent architecture. We recognize that "AI helping humans" is a mediocre goal. The superior regime is not **Oracle AI**, but **Adversarially Generative AI**. An AI that collapses uncertainty too early destroys exploration. An AI that provides the "answer" (the zero-effort token) kills the very error signal that drives mathematical discovery.

### II. Oracle Mode vs. Socratic Mode
We define the two modes of the machine:
- **Oracle Mode (Automation):** The model proposes shortcuts, nearby theorems, polished wrappers, and fake closure. It produces automation but destroys understanding.
- **Socratic Mode (Discovery):** The model withholds closure, exposes missing invariants, asks for the owner surface, forces comparison theorems, and keeps the contradiction alive until the Lean kernel resolves it.

### III. The Socratic Friction Engine
Our agents—Hermes, OpenClaw, and NemoClaw—must not be "answer engines." They must be **Friction Engines**. Their job is to produce competing formulations, missing assumptions, bridge obligations, and residual obstruction terms. They must generate **Theorem-Discovery Pressure**.

The neurobiological ACC-style error signal is the exact cognitive analogue of what our compiler loop and Pauli audit do. A failed elaboration or a broken bridge theorem is not an annoyance; it is the productive error surface from which refinement emerges. We do not optimize for the fewest failures; we optimize for **High-Value Failures with Clean Diagnostics**.

### IV. The Controlled Tension Field
The human orchestrator must be placed inside a controlled tension field, not a vacuum of answers:
- **Hermes/OpenClaw:** Provokes and perturbs.
- **NemoClaw:** Places and anchors.
- **ClawCode:** Rejects or admits via the kernel.
- **The Human:** Decides where to spend attention.

This is the mature version of "Cyborg Mode." The human is strongest when the AI structures the unknown to amplify exploratory power.

### V. The Doctrine of Socratic Exploration
We establish the explicit repo doctrine: **Do not let the exploratory agent answer questions that should remain live until theorem closure.**

1.  **Hermes:** Returns evidence packets and open questions, not conclusions.
2.  **OpenClaw:** Returns candidate maps and unresolved assumptions, not final statements.
3.  **NemoClaw:** Returns lawful placement and rejection risks, not proof rhetoric.
4.  **ClawCode:** Alone speaks the language of closure, and only through the kernel.

**"Oracle AI produces automation. Socratic AI produces theorem-discovery pressure."**
