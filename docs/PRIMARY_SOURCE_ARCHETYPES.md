## 2. Preserved `ORIGINAL_REQUEST.md § R3` Architectural Principles & `BUCKET 1/2/3` (`O-EXT`) Discipline

All Lean 4 formalizations in Milestones `M2`–`M6` MUST preserve the founders' algebraic and geometric structures without shortcuts:

1. **`R3.1` — Three-Way Separation of $J_{\text{Krein}}$, $\Gamma_{\text{grade}}$, and $J_{\text{real}}$ + Native Indefinite Pairings**:
   - Never force Euclidean Wick rotations to erase indefinite Krein signatures (`KreinSpace` in `lean/InfoGeometry/Krein/KreinSpace.lean:54–61`, `SplitDoubledSpace` in `lean/InfoGeometry/KK/ClNNFredholmBridge.lean:100–101`).
   - Strictly separate:
     - $J_{\text{Krein}}$ (`jCLM`, $\eta$, $\mathcal{J}$, $\beta$): **linear**, bounded, self-adjoint unitary involution ($J_{\text{Krein}}^2 = 1$ or $\beta^2 = -1$ in skew convention) defining $[\psi, \phi] = \langle \psi, J_{\text{Krein}} \phi \rangle$.
     - $\Gamma_{\text{grade}}$ (`gradeCLM`, $\chi$, $\gamma$): **linear** $\mathbb{Z}/2$-grading involution ($\chi^2 = 1$) governing odd/even parity and Koszul signs.
     - $J_{\text{real}}$ (`RealStructure` in `SpectralTriple.lean:107–124`, modular conjugation $J_{\text{mod}}$): **antilinear** isometry (real-linear with $JK = -KJ$ relative to Hestenes phase axis $K$) mediating opposite-algebra commutant action and mod-8 KO signs.
   - Preserve native Duhamel / Bogoliubov–Kubo–Mori canonical pairings and Casimir $J$-reciprocity ($L^T = J L J$) without collapsing odd-parity sectors.
2. **`R3.2` — Metric-Free $\mathrm{SL}(V)$-Covariant Regressive Products & Basis-Free $O(Q)$ Clifford/Witt Modules**:
   - Grassmann regressive products (`ExteriorRegressive.lean`, `GrassmannAusdehnungslehreRegressiveShadow.lean`) must remain metric-free and $\mathrm{SL}(V)$-invariant (scaling by $(\det g)^{-1}$ under $g \in \mathrm{GL}(V)$) without forcing a Euclidean Hodge dual.
   - Separate `GL(4)` syncyclicity (`IsSyncyclic4`) from Lorentzian $O(1,3)$ circle tangency (Grassmann Note 97).
   - Keep `CliffordAlgebra.lipschitzGroup Q`, $O(Q)$ invariance (`CoordinateLessCliffordInvariance.lean`), and Witt orthogonal/hyperbolic decomposition ($V_{\mathrm{aniso}} \perp \mathbb{H}^m$) basis-free without matrix coordinate assumptions.
3. **`R3.3` — Koszul $\mathbb{Z}/2$-Graded Tensor Products ($\hat{\otimes}$) & Explicit Jordan–Wigner Soldering**:
   - Clifford towers $\mathrm{Cl}(V \oplus W, Q_V \perp Q_W) \cong \mathrm{Cl}(V, Q_V) \hat{\otimes} \mathrm{Cl}(W, Q_W)$ must use Koszul signs $(-1)^{\deg(b_1)\deg(a_2)}$ (`GradedTensorProduct`, `Cl11GradedTensorMorita.lean`).

### 6.2 Verified KO-Dimension $(\epsilon, \epsilon', \epsilon'')$ & Fundamental Symmetry Sign Audit (`S3-25`..`S3-28` vs. `SpectralTriple.lean`)

Direct `pdftotext` inspection of the retrieved PDFs and `lean/InfoGeometry/OperatorAlgebra/SpectralTriple.lean:68–80, 114–125, 214–221, 312–319` establishes **four distinct conventions** that M5/M6 must map explicitly rather than conflate:

1. **Connes (1995) `reality.pdf`, Definition 3 & eq. (1.46) (p. 6204)**:
   - Covers **even** mod-8 dimensions $d = 2k \pmod 8$ with **two** signs $(\epsilon, \epsilon')$ (fixing $JD = DJ$):
     $$JD = DJ, \qquad J^2 = \epsilon, \qquad J\gamma = \epsilon' \gamma J$$
   - Table (1.46): $d = 0 \mapsto (+1, +1)$, $d = 2 \mapsto (-1, -1)$, $d = 4 \mapsto (-1, +1)$, $d = 6 \mapsto (+1, -1)$.
   - General $n \pmod 8$ is reduced in eqs. (1.47)–(1.49) via an auxiliary $\mathrm{Cl}_{p,q}$ representation $\pi(\gamma_j^E)$.
2. **Connes (1996) *CMP* 182 / Standard 3-Sign Convention (adopted verbatim in `SpectralTriple.lean:68–80` `KOSigns`)**:
   - Uses three signs $(\epsilon, \epsilon', \epsilon'')$ for all $d \in \mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$:
     - `epsJ` ($\epsilon$): $J^2 = \epsilon$ (`J.val.comp J.val = signs.epsJ • id`)
     - `epsD` ($\epsilon'$): $JD = \epsilon' DJ$ (`J.J.val.comp D.D = signs.epsD • (D.D.comp J.J.val)`)
     - `epsChi` ($\epsilon''$): $J\gamma = \epsilon'' \gamma J$ (`J.J.val.comp χ.chi = signs.epsChi • (χ.chi.comp J.J.val)`)
   - *(Note that Connes 1995's $\epsilon'$ is Connes 1996's $\epsilon''$!)*
3. **Paschke & Sitarz (2006) `math-ph/0611029`, Section 2, Definition 2.1, eqs. (3)–(4) (Lorentzian signature $(1,q)$)**:
   - **Permutes $\epsilon$ and $\epsilon'$** relative to Connes (1996) / `KOSigns`:
     $$DJ = \epsilon J D, \qquad J^2 = \epsilon', \qquad J\gamma = \epsilon'' \gamma J$$
     indexed by $1 - q \pmod 8$: $\epsilon = (+, +, +, -, +, +, +, -)$, $\epsilon' = (+, +, +, -, -, -, -, +)$, and $\epsilon'' = (+, \cdot, -, \cdot, +, \cdot, -, \cdot)$ for even $1-q$.
   - Fundamental symmetry uses the skew-adjoint convention $\beta = -\beta^\dagger, \beta^2 = -1$ (so $D^\dagger = \beta D \beta$, $\beta\gamma = -\gamma\beta$, $\beta J = -\epsilon^p J \beta$).
4. **van den Dungen, Paschke & Rennie (2013) `1207.2112`, §3.3/§3.3.1, Definition 4.4, Definition 5.8, & Remark 5.9**:
   - Definition 4.4 defines a pseudo-Riemannian spectral triple $(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{D})$ on a Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}$ (with optional grading $\Gamma$ and without $J_{\text{real}}$ or $J_{\text{Krein}}$ in the base triple); §3.3/§3.3.1 introduces the Krein fundamental symmetry $\mathcal{J} = P^+ - P^-$ / $J_M$ ($\mathcal{J}^2 = 1$), Definition 5.8 defines Lorentz-type $(\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{D}, \beta)$ with skew $\beta^2 = -1$ ($J = i\beta$), Remark 5.9 notes odd timelike parity *"can be refined using a real structure"*, and Def. 4.1 / Thm 5.1 define the Wick-rotated symmetric operator $D_E = e^{i\pi/4}(D - i D^*)/\sqrt{2}$.
