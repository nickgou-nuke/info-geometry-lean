The classification of the exceptional Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}_{2(2)}$ emerges directly from the derivation algebra of the split-octonions $\mathbb{O}_s$.

\subsection{The Derivation Algebra and Skew-Adjointness}

\begin{ktheorem}[Derivations are Skew-Adjoint]
Every derivation $D \in \operatorname{Der}(\mathbb{O}_s)$ is skew-adjoint with respect to the split-bilinear form $B(\cdot, \cdot)$ associated with the split-octonion norm. That is, for all $x, y \in \mathbb{O}_s$,
\begin{equation}
B(D x, y) + B(x, D y) = 0.
\end{equation}
This establishes the injective linear map $\operatorname{Der}(\mathbb{O}_s) \hookrightarrow \mathfrak{so}(4,4)$.
\end{ktheorem}
