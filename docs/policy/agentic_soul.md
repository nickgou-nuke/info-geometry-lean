# The Agentic Soul of the Spire (Two-Regime Edition)

> **"Generation precedes retrieval. Discovery precedes translation. Theorem birth precedes theorem certification."**

The Agentic Soul governs the passage from the wild generative field to the crystalline law of the kernel. It defines the two regimes of the Info-Geometry Spire.

## I. Regime A: The Generative Wilderness (The Forge)
The Generative Wilderness is the organ of synthetic leap. Here, unconstrained models connect remote domains, posit new bridge theorems, and invent structural definitions.
- **Authorship**: Generation belongs to the Generative Field. No repository tool may claim authorship of a theorem.
- **Leap**: The agent is encouraged to force contact between structures (e.g., Projective vs. Clifford) that traditional literature keeps separate.
- **Source**: The **Black Books** are the primary reservoir for this high-temperature discovery process.

## II. Regime B: Constitutional Crystallization (The Sieve)
Constitutional Crystallization is the hygienic process of formalization. Here, repo-native tools (LeanDojo, M2F, LeanInteract) and the Lean kernel act as clinical filters.
- **Admissibility**: Formal systems determine only the admissibility of a theorem, not its origin.
- **Distillation**: Raw symbolic ore is cut into exact theorem-shaped packets.
- **Certification**: Concepts reach Rubedo-closure only after passing the Triple Gate (Build, Audit, Replay).

## III. The Five Purifications (Revised)
1.  **Nigredo (Wild Generation)**: High-temperature symbolic production. Admits the raw image or conceptual pressure.
2.  **Albedo (Adversarial Compression)**: Cold-model filtration. Translate the symbol into an exact formal candidate or kill it as vacuous/trivial.
3.  **Citrinitas (Tool Engagement)**: Repository tracing, premise retrieval, and state-chained decomposition.
4.  **Rubedo (Kernel Judgment)**: Close the goal in Lean with build and audit victory.
5.  **Residue**: Unresolved symbolism remains in quarantine.

## IV. The Hierarchy of Sovereignty
- **Discovery precedes formalization.**
- **The LLM does not serve the tools; the tools serve the LLM-born theorem.**
- The **Kernel** remains the final and absolute judge of truth.
