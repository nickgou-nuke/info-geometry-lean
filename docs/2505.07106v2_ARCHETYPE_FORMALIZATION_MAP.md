# Archetype map for arXiv:2505.07106v2

Primary source: Filimoshina and Shirokov, “Generalized Degenerate Clifford and
Lipschitz Groups in Geometric Algebras,” [arXiv:2505.07106v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.07106).
The local source reviewed was
`/home/goutev/Downloads/collection_for_formalization/2505.07106v2 (10).pdf`.
The paper studies Clifford algebras `Cl_{p,q,r}`, including degenerate forms,
and characterizes generalized Clifford and Lipschitz groups by centralizers,
twisted centralizers, and norm maps.

## Archetypes and prerequisite order

The arrows below mean “is mathematical input to”; they do not assert an
isomorphism or equivalence between nodes.

```text
Clifford carrier and quadratic relation
                 ↓
grade involution + reversion
                 ↓
four joint sign subspaces
             ↙       ↘
ordinary and twisted   reversion and Clifford
centralizers           norm maps
             ↘       ↙
        stabilizer/group characterizations
                 ↓
       group identities and inclusions
          ↙             ↘
degenerate examples   Lie algebras/dimensions
```

The formal dependency relation is a finite partial order in
`InfoGeometry.Clifford.GeneralizedDegenerateLipschitzArchetypes`. Centralizers
and norm maps are incomparable peers: each supplies a distinct input to the
stabilizer characterization. The listed order is a prerequisite model, not a
claim that every statement in a later paper section depends on every earlier
node.

The theorem `clifford_finrank_eq_two_pow` proves, for an arbitrary real
quadratic form on a finite-dimensional vector space, that the full Clifford
carrier has rank `2^(finrank ℝ M)`. The theorem
`fixedGradeCarrier_finrank_eq_choose` proves, in the same generality, that
`finrank ℝ (Cl^k) = Nat.choose (finrank ℝ M) k`. Its proof transports the
grade projection to the exterior-algebra grade projection, identifies the
projection range with the `k`th exterior power, and applies Mathlib's
exterior-power rank theorem. This establishes signature-independent carrier
and grade ranks, including for degenerate forms. It is foundational dimension
data only; it does not prove the paper's stabilizer Lie-algebra dimension
tables in Appendix B. The signature-specific rank table is complemented by
`degenerateSignature_fixedGradeCarrier_eq_bot_of_gt_dimension`, which proves
that every grade `k > p + q + r` vanishes for every signature triple; the
`Cl_{0,0,2}` grade-three example now reuses this general carrier theorem.

## Exact Lean theorem and scope

The core theorem is
`adjointAction_mem_reverseFixed_iff_norm_commutes`. For a unit `g` of
`CliffordAlgebra Q` and `x` satisfying `reverse x = x`, it proves

```text
reverse (g * x * g⁻¹) = g * x * g⁻¹
  ↔ reverse(g) * g * x = x * reverse(g) * g.
```

The result uses only the anti-multiplicativity and involutivity of reversion
and the two-sided inverse laws for `g`. It does not require `Q` to be
nondegenerate. The companion theorem
`stabilizes_reverseFixed_iff_reversionNormCentralizes` states the corresponding
setwise stabilizer equality. A second ordinary-action theorem treats the
reverse-anti-fixed set. The grade-twisted action has reverse-fixed and
reverse-anti-fixed iff criteria using the Clifford norm and twisted
centralizers, and classifies both corresponding joint-pair stabilizers.

This is a common algebraic mechanism behind the paper’s norm/centralizer
characterizations. The owner distinguishes actual homogeneous grade sums from
joint-involution eigenspace pairs. The new joint-eigenspace results
`jointPair_01_eq_reverseFixed`, `jointPair_23_eq_reverseAntiFixed`, and
`jointPair_03_eq_starFixed` prove the corresponding decompositions over `ℝ`;
the last result is only a carrier identity and does not identify the paper's
type-three centralizer with the pair `0,3` centralizer. The theorem
`adjointPairStabilizer_01_eq_ReverseFixedNormCarrier`
and `adjointPairStabilizer_23_eq_ReverseAntiFixedNormCarrier` classify those
two ordinary-adjoint stabilizers for arbitrary real quadratic forms.
`adjointPairStabilizer_01_eq_PaperA01` proves the `A^{01}` classification.
`PaperA01_subset_PaperA23` and `PaperB12_subset_PaperB03` prove the two
ordinary group inclusions in Theorem 4.1, using the fact that commuting with
grade one means commuting with the entire Clifford algebra. In this owner,
`fixedGradeCarrier` denotes the homogeneous-grade carrier, while
`jointTypeSubmodule` denotes a separate simultaneous grade-involution and
reversion eigenspace. Accordingly, `PaperA23` is the reversion-norm preimage
of the intersection of the homogeneous grade-2 and grade-3 centralizers, and
`PaperB03` is the Clifford-norm preimage of the homogeneous grade-3
centralizer, matching the paper's notation. The distinct
`JointTypeA23NormCarrier` retains the reversion-norm preimage of the joint
quaternion-type centralizer. Its theorem
`adjointPairStabilizer_23_eq_JointTypeA23NormCarrier` classifies that
joint-sector stabilizer only; it is not the paper's homogeneous-grade
`A^{23}` classification. No general geometric classification for the
paper's `A^{23}` or `B^{03}` is claimed yet.
The inclusions `fixedGradeCarrier_two_subset_jointType_two` and
`fixedGradeCarrier_three_subset_jointType_three` yield the norm-carrier
inclusions `JointTypeA23NormCarrier_subset_PaperA23` and
`JointTypeB03NormCarrier_subset_PaperB03`. They prove one direction of the
centralizer comparison; they do not prove the reverse centralizer inclusions
or the paper's full geometric group equalities.
Composing these carrier inclusions with the already proved stabilizer
criteria gives `adjointPairStabilizer_23_subset_PaperA23`; the star-fixed
carrier criterion and grade-three inclusion give
`adjointPairStabilizer_03_subset_PaperB03`. These are one-sided group
inclusions, with the reverse directions still open.
The paper's notation table distinguishes `Cl⁰` (homogeneous scalar grade)
from `Cl⁽⁰⁾` (the sum of even grades). Accordingly, `PaperACheck03` and
`PaperBCheck01` constrain their norm values to `evenCarrier`, characterized
by grade-involution fixedness and proved equivalent to Mathlib's `evenOdd Q 0`;
they do not use the scalar-grade carrier. The paper's `B̌^{01}` entry uses
the ordinary grade-one centralizer intersected with the even subspace. The
theorems `reverseFixedTwistedCentralizer_eq_gradeOne_even` and
`twistedPairStabilizer_01_eq_PaperBCheck01` establish the corresponding
geometric classification for arbitrary real quadratic forms.
For the checked `Ǎ^{03}` case, `gradeTwistedAdjoint_mem_starFixed_iff_reversionNorm_twistedCentralizes`
and `twistedPairStabilizer_03_eq_StarFixedTwistedNormCarrier` prove that the
`0⊕3` grade-twisted stabilizer is exactly the reversion-norm preimage of the
twisted centralizer of the star-fixed pair. Identifying that carrier with the
paper's `Z^3 ∩ Cl⁽⁰⁾` requires the homogeneous-grade/quaternion-type centralizer
reduction, which remains open. The reusable lemma
`twistedCentralizer_starFixed_eq_even_inter_centralizer_starFixed` now shows
that this twisted centralizer is exactly the even part intersected with the
ordinary centralizer of the star-fixed sector; the group-level form is
`twistedPairStabilizer_03_eq_StarFixedEvenCentralizerNormCarrier`. This makes
the parity constraint explicit without claiming the still-missing reduction
to the paper's grade-three centralizer.
Theorem 4.2's checked inclusion `B̌^{01} ⊆ B̌^{23}` is proved as
`PaperBCheck01_subset_PaperBCheck23`: the first carrier forces its Clifford
norm to be even and central, placing it in both grade-twisted centralizers
that define the second carrier.
`jointPair_12_eq_starAntiFixed` identifies the 1⊕2 carrier with the
Clifford-star anti-fixed subspace. The theorem
`adjointAction_mem_starAntiFixed_iff_cliffordNorm_commutes` proves that the
ordinary adjoint action preserves an element of that sector exactly when the
Clifford norm commutes with it. Together with
`quaternionPairCentralizer_12_eq_gradeOne`, this yields
`adjointPairStabilizer_12_eq_PaperB12`, the `B^{12}` classification from
Theorem 4.1 for arbitrary real quadratic forms. The joint-type `0⊕3`
stabilizer has the star-fixed centralizer characterization described below.
For `Cl_{0,0,2}`, `radicalPlane_starFixed_eq_scalarRange` proves that this
sector is scalar, yielding the full action-level `B^{03}` classification via
`radicalPlane_adjointPairStabilizer_03_eq_PaperB03`. Identifying that
centralizer with the homogeneous grade-three centralizer for arbitrary forms
remains open. The other norm-table
entries also await their general geometric group equalities. The grade-twisted stabilizer of joint pair `0,1` is now classified
at the action level by `twistedPairStabilizer_01_iff_cliffordNorm_twistedCentralizes_reverseFixed`;
the complementary pair `2,3` is classified by
`twistedPairStabilizer_23_iff_cliffordNorm_twistedCentralizes_reverseAntiFixed`.
The `0⊕1` case is identified with `B̌^{01}` by
`twistedPairStabilizer_01_eq_PaperBCheck01`. For `B̌^{23}`,
`homogeneousGradePairTwistedCentralizer_eq_inter` packages the intersection of
the two grade-twisted centralizers as twisted centralization of the sum of
homogeneous grades 2 and 3, and
`PaperBCheck23_eq_homogeneousGradePairTwistedCentralizer_preimage` records the
corresponding norm preimage. Identifying this carrier with the
reverse-anti-fixed-sector stabilizer remains open at the homogeneous-grade
level. The geometric side is separately classified by
`twistedPairStabilizer_23_eq_quaternionPairTwistedCentralizer_preimage`, and
`quaternionPairTwistedCentralizer_eq_inter` expresses that carrier as the
intersection of the two joint-type twisted centralizers. The remaining step is
the paper's Lemma 3.2 identification between these grade-indexed and
joint-type carriers.
`reverseFixedCentralizer_eq_fixedGradeCentralizer_one`
proves the key grade-one centralizer reduction for the `0⊕1` sector.
`homogeneousGradePairCentralizer_*` concerns the separately defined
homogeneous-grade carriers. The owner now proves
`quaternionTypeCentralizer_one_eq_fixedGradeCentralizer_one`: in grade one,
the homogeneous-grade and quaternion-type ordinary centralizers agree,
because grade one is contained in type one and its centralizer is the full
algebra center. The grade-two and grade-three ordinary comparisons and the
twisted comparisons needed by the tables remain open; no general
identification of those carriers is assumed. Remaining work includes the other norm-table equalities,
twisted and modified-twisted pair classifications, group products/inclusions,
signature-specific centralizer reductions, and Lie algebra dimension tables.

The norm-sector statement (3.20) is also formalized as
`reversionNorm_mem_jointPair_01` and `cliffordNorm_mem_jointPair_03`, using the
proved carrier decompositions and the star-fixed norm identity. This establishes
the target sectors for the two norm maps; it does not by itself classify the
corresponding adjoint stabilizers. For the ordinary adjoint action, the
additional theorem `adjointPairStabilizer_03_eq_StarFixedNormCarrier` identifies
the `0⊕3` stabilizer with the Clifford-norm centralizer of the star-fixed
joint-type sector. In `Cl_{0,0,2}`, `radicalPlane_starFixed_eq_scalarRange`
identifies that sector with scalars, so
`radicalPlane_adjointPairStabilizer_03_eq_PaperB03` proves the paper's carrier
equality in this example. The arbitrary-form equality with the homogeneous
grade-three centralizer remains open.

The low-dimensional degenerate example `radicalLine_ordinary_group_classifications`
instantiates the paper's `Cl_{0,0,1}` on `ℝ` with zero quadratic form. It uses
Mathlib's `CliffordAlgebraDualNumber.equiv` to prove the algebra is commutative;
therefore ordinary adjoint action is the identity, and the geometric groups
for pairs `0⊕1`, `2⊕3`, `1⊕2`, and `0⊕3` equal the corresponding paper norm
carriers in this particular one-dimensional radical model. The equality is
degenerate (all four carriers and stabilizers are the full unit group) and
does not establish the general centralizer comparison of Lemma 3.2. The
additional results `radicalLine_fixedGradeCarrier_eq_bot`
and `radicalLine_PaperBCheck23_eq_univ` prove that grades two and three vanish
and the checked `B̌^{23}` norm carrier is also the full unit group.
`radicalLine_PaperBCheck01_eq_univ` adds the analogous full-unit
classification for the checked `B̌^{01}` carrier: the Clifford norm's
star-fixed sector gives parity-evenness because reversion is the identity in
this commutative one-generator algebra, while commutativity supplies the
grade-one centralizer condition. `radicalLine_fixedGradeTwistedCentralizer_one_eq_univ`
and `radicalLine_PaperACheck12_eq_univ` establish the paper's ordinary
`Ǎ^{12}` carrier is also the full unit group in this radical-line instance:
the grade-one twisted condition follows from its one-dimensional span, and
the grade-two test is vacuous because that homogeneous carrier is zero. This
is a signature-specific carrier equality, not evidence for the general
centralizer comparison in Lemma 3.2.
The theorems `radicalLine_PaperQCheck01_eq_univ`,
`radicalLine_PaperQCheck23_eq_univ`,
`radicalLine_PaperQCheck12_eq_univ`, and
`radicalLine_PaperQCheck03_eq_univ` prove all four modified twisted-carrier
identities in equation (6.15) for `Cl_{0,0,1}`. The `Q̃^{23}` and `Q̃^{03}`
tests vanish above dimension one; `Q̃^{01}` uses evenness of the Clifford
norm and commutativity. The `Q̃^{12}` proof identifies the grade-one carrier
with the span of its generator using `fixedGradeCarrier_finrank_eq_choose`,
then proves the required twisted commutation by Clifford induction and the
zero product of radical generators. These are exact results for the
one-dimensional radical model, not higher-dimensional radical Grassmann
classifications.
`radicalLine_evenCarrier_eq_scalarRange` proves that the even subalgebra is
the scalar subalgebra in this model, and
`radicalLine_PaperACheck03_eq_scalarNormCarrier` therefore characterizes the
checked `Ǎ^{03}` carrier by a scalar-valued reversion norm.
`radicalLine_unit_sq_scalar_implies_scalar` proves that a unit with scalar
square is scalar, which yields `radicalLine_PaperACheck03_eq_scalarUnits`, the
paper's low-dimensional scalar-unit classification.
`radicalLine_twistedPairStabilizer_03_eq_PaperACheck03` then identifies the
grade-twisted geometric stabilizer of joint types `0⊕3` with this checked
group. This is a signature-specific equality in `Cl_{0,0,1}`; it does not
prove the corresponding centralizer reduction or group classification for
general signatures. Other full twisted-action coincidences remain unproved.

## Repository context checked

The existing Mathlib-backed `LipschitzVectorAction` owner concerns the standard
vector action of the usual Lipschitz group. It is a different level from the
paper’s generalized stabilizers of fixed-grade subspaces. Existing Clifford
conjugation APIs supply the involutions used here. The new module is therefore
a leaf formalization of the shared norm/stabilizer lemma, not a replacement for
the existing vector-action owner.
