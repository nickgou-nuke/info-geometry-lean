The Pauli auditor is not historical ornament in this repository.
It is a current epistemic discipline.

## Core Rule

Before a candidate reaches theorem authority, it should be subjected to the
Pauli question:

```text
is this even true?
```

In repo terms this means:

- do not let language outrun mathematics
- do not let wrappers impersonate closure
- do not let generated structure impersonate proof
- do not let symbolic richness bypass owner, build, or audit discipline

## Role In The Repository

The repository works by tension between three live forces:

1. Jungian generation
   - expansion of possibility
   - symbolic excitation
   - new theorem pressure
2. Pauli differentiation
   - exclusion of inflation
   - sharpening of candidate shape
   - rejection of decorative closure
3. Lean / build authority
   - final verification
   - theorem integration

Pauli is therefore a pre-authority filter.
It does not replace Lean, but the repo depends on it to prevent false ascent.

## Current Meaning Of “Not Even True”

The repo’s stronger internal warning is not only “not even wrong.”
It is often:

```text
not even true
```

This phrase names a live repository instinct:

- the statement may sound deep
- the corridor may sound elegant
- the bridge may feel natural
- but if it is not derived, not mapped to owners, or not surviving direct
  verification, it does not count
