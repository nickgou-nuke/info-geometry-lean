**Topic**: `Unipotent Weyl units and Jordan multiplication closure from square-zero Peirce pairs`

Hi everyone,

While working on noncommutative operator algebra in Lean 4 (`v4.34.1`), we formalized a division-free construction of unipotent Weyl reflection units, weighted trace identities, and Jordan multiplication closure generated by a square-zero Moore–Penrose / Peirce pair `(d, B)` (`d^2 = 0`, `B^2 = 0`, `d * B * d = d`, `B * d * B = B`) in an arbitrary associative `Ring A`:

1. **Unipotent Weyl unit in `Aˣ` without division by `2`:**
   From the unipotent transvections `U_+(d) = 1 + d` and `U_-(B) = 1 - B`, the triple product
   $$W = (1 + d)(1 - B)(1 + d) = 1 - (dB + Bd) + d - B$$
   is an explicit unit in `Aˣ` with inverse `W⁻¹ = (1 - d)(1 + B)(1 - d)`, satisfying the exact Weyl conjugation laws:
   - `W * (d * B) * W⁻¹ = B * d` and `W * (B * d) * W⁻¹ = d * B`
   - `W * d * W⁻¹ = -B` and `W * B * W⁻¹ = -d`
   - `W^2 = 1 - 2 * (d * B + B * d)` (acts as `-1` on the active Peirce support `Δ = dB + Bd` and `+1` on `1 - Δ`), and `(Δ + W)^6 = Δ`.
   - The transvections are recovered directly from the commutator `Γ = dB - Bd` and `W` via `(1 + Γ) * W * (1 - Γ) = 2 * d` and `(1 - Γ) * W * (1 + Γ) = -2 * B`.

2. **Jordan multiplication as native `Module.End R A` maps:**
   Using the symmetrized Jordan product `L_a(x) = a * x + x * a` as a native `R`-linear endomorphism (`Module.End R A`), the commutator `[L_a, L_b]` is the associative inner derivation `x ↦ [x, [a, b]]`, satisfying the Leibniz rule on `A` and sending `(d, B)` to `-2 • d` and `2 • B` under the associative Jordan associator `[L_d, L_B](d)`.
