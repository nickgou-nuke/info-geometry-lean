an ambient homeomorphism action from being selected through subgroup

## Corrections and boundaries

- The invariant carrier is part of the closure. A volume invariant bounds the
  closure formed using **measures**. A closure using only fields or flows
  cannot use that measure without an additional construction.
- `Stab({μ}) = SDiff(E, μ)` does not imply that the closure of every
  volume-preserving flow subgroup is all of SDiff. The proved target is
  containment, which permits additional common invariants.
- A flow stabilizer is a group centralizer. A field stabilizer is defined by
  derivative pushforward; it is not a centralizer of elements of `Diff(E)`.
  The converse from field invariance to flow commutation needs uniqueness of
  integral curves and is not silently assumed.
- A real-time group homomorphism need not be continuous or differentiable.
  The derivative theorem states its regularity hypothesis. General
  time-dependent Euler trajectories need not be one-parameter subgroups.
- Unrelated measure, contact, symplectic, metric, and flow stabilizers do not
  form the unconditional nested chain suggested in the intake. Intersections
  express the correct adjoining-invariants construction.
- Orbit quotients here are coset spaces. No normality, quotient-group,
  quotient-manifold, or transitivity on all densities is inferred.
- Lebesgue-measure preservation allows orientation reversal. A general
  logarithmic Jacobian is a cocycle, not a character on all diffeomorphisms.
  Constant scaling on a finite nonzero measure is already rigid in the
  density-orbit owner.
- Mathlib's field bracket is `DX(Y) - DY(X)` for `[Y,X]`. Differentiating
  forward pushforward along the flow of `Y` has the opposite sign to that
  bracket; a Lie derivative convention must specify inverse flow.
- A single abstract flow has an abelian image, not necessarily a copy of the
  real additive group. The concrete Arnold flow separately has a proved
  injectivity theorem.
- Setwise preservation of a real linear span of fields is not automatically
  a foliation or PDE symmetry theorem. Lie bracket generation alone supplies
  no Chow–Rashevskii controllability theorem.
- Volume preservation alone does not preserve a chosen Euclidean metric,
  curl operator, or the Euclidean Euler equation under arbitrary coordinate
  pushforward. The bridge does not add such a covariance claim.
