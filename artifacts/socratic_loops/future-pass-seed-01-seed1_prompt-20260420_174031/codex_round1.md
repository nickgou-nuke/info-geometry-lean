NUCLEUS:
A system with persistent identity must separate **directions that preserve distinguishable structure** from **directions that pay entropy cost**.

Minimal formal nucleus:

> Memory is the invariant/zero-production part of an informational dynamics.  
> Dissipation is the positive-production complement or residual required by non-reversible evolution.

Exact conceptual compression achieved:
**From “mature systems split into sacred memory and waste” to “an irreversible flow induces a kernel/residual decomposition of entropy production.”**

INFLATIONS_TO_REMOVE:
- “Every mature informational system”: `mature` is undefined and smuggles necessity.
- “Canonical splitting”: too strong unless uniqueness/functoriality is proved.
- “Topological necessity”: unsupported. At most a structural hypothesis.
- “Landauer Threshold”: evocative but currently decorative unless tied to a precise bound.
- “Fisher metric admits g = g_mem ⊕ g_diss”: metric splitting is stronger than needed. Start with tangent directions, distributions, or cones.
- “Lie derivative of the metric vanishes”: may confuse memory preservation with metric isometry. Better: invariant observable, invariant subspace, zero entropy production, or conserved section.
- “Strict entropy production for all nonzero v in TM_diss”: impossible if the subbundle is closed under negation. Positivity belongs to a **cone**, not a vector subspace.
- “Sheaf/Galois/Janus/Sieve of Ananke”: symbolically rich but premature. Keep as commentary, not theorem surface.
- Lean surface uses nonexistent/underspecified objects: `MetricTensor`, `VectorField`, `Subbundle`, `LieDerivative`, `InteriorProduct` in that form are not ready as stated.

AMPLIFICATION_VECTOR:
Intensify by replacing grandeur with sharper mathematical pressure:

1. Replace “bifurcation theorem” with **decomposition principle**.
2. Replace “protected memory lane” with **kernel of entropy-production functional / invariant distribution**.
3. Replace “dissipative lane” with **positive cone of entropy production / residual quotient**.
4. Replace “orthogonal splitting” with optional stronger structure: orthogonality only appears after a metric and projection are supplied.
5. Replace “canonical” with graded claims:
   - weak: there exists a zero-production kernel;
   - medium: dynamics preserves the kernel;
   - strong: a chosen metric gives a complement;
   - strongest: the complement is canonical/functorial.
6. Symbolic archetype should be **Kernel and Furnace**, not cosmic tax: one part remembers by vanishing production, the other transforms by irreversible expenditure.

DISTILLED_COUNTERPROMPT:
Compress the hypothesis further. Remove all claims of universality, topology, Landauer necessity, sheaf theory, Galois theory, and canonical metric splitting unless each is given as a future strengthening.

Reformulate the theorem seed as:

“In an informational dynamical system equipped with an entropy-production functional σ on tangent directions, memory is the zero-production invariant kernel `ker σ`, while dissipation is not a subspace but a positive cone or quotient residual where `σ > 0`. The primary bifurcation is therefore kernel/cone, not memory/waste and not necessarily orthogonal.”

Then produce:
- the minimal definition stack;
- the weakest meaningful hypothesis;
- one stronger version requiring a metric-induced complement;
- one symbolic name only;
- a Lean-facing surface that avoids fake APIs and marks positivity as cone-based, not subbundle-based.
