Full finite real matrices, existing KasparovKreinData K, arbitrary P with
P*P=P and K.diracAdjoint P=P. Use native ranges of component10Linear and
component01Linear from Core.PeirceMatrixPerfectPairing. Restrict existing
Dirac adjunction to a REAL linear equivalence between the opposite corners;
involution supplies the inverse. Pull back the existing perfect trace pairing
along this equivalence in the second argument using LinearMap.compl₁₂ and
mathlib IsPerfPair.compl₁₂. The resulting actual pairing evaluates to
trace(X * K.diracAdjoint Y), and toPerfPair gives the corner/dual equivalence.
No positivity, proper-star or coordinate restriction; real coefficients are
intentional because general complex Dirac adjunction is conjugate-linear.
