Native Zorn split-polar adjoint, not positive Hilbert adjoint. Reuse ketBra_adjoint, ketBra_mul and polar_adjoint_unique. Prove products dB and Bd are adjoint partners for d=ketXX B=ketYY; their sum support is selfadjoint. Under null X and cross normalization, dB differs from Bd, hence dB is not selfadjoint. This supplies an explicit obstruction to treating this reflexive pair as a polar-MP pair. No global StarRing instance or positive adjoint is introduced.
