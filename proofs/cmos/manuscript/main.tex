\begin{abstract}
This monograph establishes a rigorous, unified framework bridging exceptional Lie algebras, information geometry, and differentiable inverse problems for nuclear spectroscopy. We cleanly distinguish between theorems verified in the Lean 4 kernel, mathematical constructions, empirical results, and physical interpretations. The architecture proceeds from the thermodynamic foundations of Gibbs variational geometry and Optimal Transport (Sinkhorn), ascends into the algebraic classification of the split-octonion derivation algebra ($\mathfrak{g}_{2(2)}$) and its Triality ($\mathfrak{so}(4,4)$), embeds into the Tits-Kantor-Koecher construction, and finally maps these mathematical symmetries onto a high-performance PyTorch factorization engine for $\gamma-\gamma$ coincidence spectrometry.
\end{abstract}
