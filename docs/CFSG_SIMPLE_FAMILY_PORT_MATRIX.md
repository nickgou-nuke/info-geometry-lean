# CFSG simple-family local port matrix

Status checked against the current checkout on 2026-10-30. `external_refs/CFSG`
is source evidence only: the root project remains on its existing Lean and
Mathlib pins, and the external declarations are not part of the root import
graph. A local entry counts as ported only when a repo-owned Lean theorem has
the stated conclusion on the corresponding local carrier and its proof is
kernel checked.

## Local public import surface

`lean/InfoGeometry.lean` imports `InfoGeometry.Exceptional.CFSGPort.All`.
That umbrella imports `SimpleFamilyCatalog`, `ClassicalSimpleFamilyCatalog`,
`ExceptionalModelSimplicity`, `SporadicGroups` transitively through
`ModelCatalog`, and the root-operator owners. Thus the models are exposed by
the root entrypoint, but import reachability does not mean every model has a
local simplicity theorem.

counted as additional group-family proofs.

The raw no-ignore scan found `IsSimpleGroup` text in 29,946 CFSG files and 24
openai-math files. This is a lexical count, not a theorem count: it includes
supporting hypotheses and repeated instances. A further scan found only
Mathlib copies, examples, or assumed-simplicity statements in the other
external submodule trees. The local source scan found 104 occurrences under
`lean/`; those likewise require declaration-level interpretation and are not
104 distinct simple groups.

## Symmetry census boundary

The broader inventory remains a high-recall declaration index rather than a
complete semantic census. It currently covers 27,356 tracked Lean files and
16,802 declaration candidates, including group actions, Weyl and root-system
actions, automorphism groups, operator-unit realizations, and permutation
carriers. Distinct symmetry families still require source-level tracing of
definitions, laws, imports, and dependents. The counts are not counts of
distinct groups.
