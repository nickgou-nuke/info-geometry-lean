NUCLEUS:
A mature informational dynamics separates what must be preserved from what must be spent.

Minimal form:

> An informational flow admits an invariant decomposition into a conservative lane, where distinguishability/history is preserved, and a dissipative lane, where distinguishability is monotonically lost through entropy production.

Exact conceptual compression achieved:

> **Memory–Dissipation Bifurcation → Conservative–Dissipative Invariant Splitting**

This is the strongest kernel. “Memory” is not mystic storage; it is the zero-dissipation, invariant, norm-preserving, index-bearing part. “Dissipation” is not decay in general; it is the strictly entropy-producing or contractive part.

INFLATIONS_TO_REMOVE:
- “Every mature informational system” is too universal. Replace with conditional hypotheses on a flow/operator.
- “Unique, canonical decomposition” is unsupported unless a projection, spectral theorem, Wold theorem, or ergodic decomposition supplies uniqueness.
- “Krein-doubled carrier” may be repo-native, but it is not conceptually necessary to the theorem nucleus.
- “Wold-Witten” is premature name fusion. Wold decomposition and Witten index are different mathematical technologies.
- “Topological Residue,” “Berry Phase,” “Perelman-W,” “MaxEnt,” “Radon-Nikodym Void,” and “Observer/Observed duality” are too many symbolic imports. They should not all appear in the theorem surface.
- “Q is an involution on memory and strict contraction on dissipation” is a very strong special case, not the general nucleus.
- “Bifurcation K-theory,” “Gated Functors,” and “event horizon of complexity” are false grandeur unless backed by a precise obstruction, index, category, or boundary condition.
- “Entropy-producing lane” needs a named functional. Entropy production must be expressed as monotonicity, strict decrease/increase, or Lyapunov dissipation.

AMPLIFICATION_VECTOR:
Intensify by narrowing.

Use one mathematical spine:

> A semigroup/operator has an invariant splitting into a maximal reversible/isometric sector and a strictly dissipative sector measured by a Lyapunov entropy functional.

Possible disciplined anchors:
- Wold decomposition for isometry/contraction splitting.
- Jacobs-de Leeuw-Glicksberg decomposition for reversible vs stable parts of semigroups.
- Decoherence-free subspace vs dissipative complement.
- Lyapunov/entropy production functional distinguishing zero-production from positive-production modes.
- Spectral projection: peripheral spectrum as memory, interior spectrum as dissipation.

Symbolic archetype to preserve:

> **The Janus Gate** as commentary only: one face preserves invariant identity; the other spends excess structure into entropy.

Future mathematics demanded:

> a theorem relating invariant projections, entropy production, and spectral/ergodic decomposition.

DISTILLED_COUNTERPROMPT:
Compress the hypothesis further. Remove all grand names unless they perform a precise mathematical role.

Given an informational flow or semigroup `T_t` on a structured state space, equipped with an entropy/Lyapunov functional `S`, identify hypotheses under which the space decomposes into two invariant parts:

1. a memory sector where `T_t` is reversible/isometric and entropy production is zero;
2. a dissipative sector where `T_t` is strictly contractive or has strictly positive entropy production.

Do not claim uniqueness or canonicity unless the needed projection/spectral/ergodic theorem is named. Treat “Janus Gate” only as the symbolic condensation of the split: preservation versus expenditure. Produce a Lean-adjacent theorem surface with explicit parameters: state space, flow/operator, entropy functional, invariant subspaces, and the exact preservation/dissipation predicates.
