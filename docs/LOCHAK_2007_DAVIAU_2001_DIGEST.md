## 5. Convention Alignments & Source Errata Verified Before Formalization

3. **Matrix Product vs. Lie Bracket in Eq. (36) (Daviau 2001, p. 158):**
   Printed Eq. 4. **Elimination of `x_4 = i c t` (Lochak 2007, p. 127):**
   Lochak uses von Neumann $\gamma_\mu$ matrices with imaginary time $x_4 = i c t$ and signature $(-,+,+,+)$ in Eq. (39) ($-J_\mu J^\mu = \Sigma_\mu \Sigma^\mu = \Omega_1^2 + \Omega_2^2$). 5. **Antisymmetrized Clifford Bivector in Daviau Eq. (58) (`F_{μν}` at `μ = ν`):**
