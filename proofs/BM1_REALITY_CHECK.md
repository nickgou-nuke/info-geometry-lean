## 🎯 REVISED B(M1) UNDERSTANDING:

### What the paper actually says:

> "Magnetic dipole transitions are typically dominated by the **isovector** component"

> "For strong transitions (≈1 W.u), the isovector dominates by ~2 orders of magnitude"

> "But these transitions are weaker: B(M1)_total ≈ 0.03 W.u, so conditions for isovector dominance may not apply"

**Key physics:**
- B(M1) has IS and IV components: M = M_IS + T_z × M_IV
- For mirror nuclei: B(M1)± = |M_IS ± M_IV|²
- If M_IS ≈ 0: B(M1)+ ≈ B(M1)- → **ratio ≈ 1**

### Why A=47 has M_IS ≈ 0:

The paper suggests:
1. **fp-shell configurations** - different from sd-shell
2. **Weak transition** (0.03 W.u vs typical 0.4 W.u)
3. **Specific nuclear structure** - first excited 7/2⁻ state
