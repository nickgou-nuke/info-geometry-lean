\begin{abstract}
This document records a proposed phenomenological chain relating nuclear-chirality observables to a scalar triple-product readout. The carrier equations, the proposed $\mathrm{Cl}(5,5)$ and gauge-field interpretations, the Einstein--Cartan/Nieh--Yan identification, the QRPA and instanton relations, and the isotope-specific parameter values are hypotheses in this draft, not results established by the Lean calculations. The formalized code proves finite scalar identities under explicit assumptions; it does not derive a nuclear Hamiltonian, a topological anomaly, a spectral gap, or a fit to experimental data. Each proposed implication is retained below as a research target with its required missing input stated explicitly.
\end{abstract}

\section{I. Introduction}
Spontaneous symmetry breaking in finite quantum systems requires careful delineation between mean-field artifacts and true laboratory observables. In nuclear structure, the Frauendorf-Meng tilted axis cranking (TAC) model \cite{Frauendorf1997} posited that a triaxial core coupled to high-$j$ valence particles and holes stabilizes a 3D aplanar vector tripod: $\vec{J} = \vec{R} + \vec{j}_\pi + \vec{j}_\nu$.

The scalar triple product $\vec{R} \cdot (\vec{j}_\pi \times \vec{j}_\nu)$ is a candidate order parameter for a chosen time-reversal-rotation operation. The physical interpretation of this quantity, the expected transition-rate relation, and the proposed explanation of non-degeneracy require a specified nuclear model and evidence. The cited experimental papers are background references; this draft does not establish an Einstein--Cartan or Nieh--Yan origin of the observed behavior.

\section{II. Twin Algebra and $\mathrm{Cl}(5,5)$ Krein Geometry}
The twin-vector labels and Nambu--Gorkov pseudospin are candidate modeling choices. A derivation of the RPA normalization from a Krein structure requires an explicit state space and inner product. Likewise, identifying QPNM with a Cartan grading, or the rotating-frame term with an $SU(2)$ connection and axial chemical potential, requires defined representations and interaction terms; none is established by the scalar calculations recorded here.

\section{III. Anomalous Axial Back-Reaction}
The following axial-current and Wess--Zumino--Witten terms are proposed ansätze. Deriving them would require a fermionic action, a regulator, and an explicit integration over the stated degrees of freedom. In the candidate model, one may define $\vec{J}_5 \approx \kappa(\vec{j}_\pi - \vec{j}_\nu)$ and a background parameter $\vec{B}_5 = \kappa ( \vec{J}_\pi^{(0)} - \vec{J}_\nu^{(0)} )$, coupled to a tilt variable $\theta$ by:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{H}_{\text{axial}} = \epsilon_{\text{axial}} \, \hat{\tau}_3^{\text{chiral}}, \quad \epsilon_{\text{axial}} = \vec{\omega}_{\text{coll}} \cdot \vec{B}_5
\end{equation}
The proposed effective potential is
\begin{equation}
V_{\text{eff}}(\theta) = -\frac{a}{2} \theta^2 + \frac{b}{4} \theta^4 + \epsilon_{\text{axial}} \theta.
\end{equation}
This polynomial is a candidate ansatz, not a derived consequence of the preceding definitions. Its coefficients and stability properties need to be obtained from a specified action.

\section{IV. Emergent Spacetime and the Nieh-Yan Anomaly}
The scalar $\mathcal{V}_{\text{trial}} = \vec{\omega}_{\text{coll}} \cdot (\vec{j}_\pi \times \vec{j}_\nu)$ is a coordinate scalar readout. Interpreting it as a source in curved spacetime would require constructing a metric, tetrad, connection, and matter coupling; this draft supplies none of those constructions.

The proposed torsion ansatz $\Sigma^{0ij} \propto \epsilon^{ijk} (\vec{j}_\pi \times \vec{j}_\nu)_k$ is not derived from a specified spin current. The coordinate contraction below is retained as a candidate scalar-energy readout, not as a consequence of Einstein--Cartan field equations:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{E}_{\text{anom}} = \omega_\mu T^{0\mu}_{\text{chiral}} = \vec{\omega}_{\text{coll}} \cdot (\vec{j}_\pi \times \vec{j}_\nu)
\end{equation}
The scalar triple product is not itself the Nieh--Yan invariant. Relating it to an integrated axial-current divergence would require the differential-form fields, normalization, regulator, integration domain, and boundary conditions. No nonzero $\epsilon_{\text{axial}}$ or anomaly follows from the algebraic contraction alone.
The proposed source relation $\epsilon_{\text{axial}}\propto\int_{\text{droplet}}\nabla_\mu J_5^\mu$ is retained as a hypothesis; its measure, boundary term, and coupling to the effective Hamiltonian must be supplied before it defines an energy bias.

\section{V. Microscopic QRPA and the Massive Gauge Gap}
As a proposed phenomenological relation, consider the following dispersion formula. A derivation requires a specified QRPA matrix and a calculation of its secular determinant; neither is supplied in this draft:
\begin{equation}
\hbar\Omega = \sqrt{(\hbar\Omega_0)^2 + \epsilon_{\text{axial}}^2}
\end{equation}
If this relation is assumed, the right-hand side is positive only when $\hbar\Omega_0$ and $\epsilon_{\text{axial}}$ are not both zero. The following transition ratio is a second model hypothesis, not a consequence established here:
The proposed unperturbed scale is $\hbar\Omega_0=\sqrt{\mathcal A^2-\mathcal B^2}$, conditional on a specified QRPA matrix and its stability conditions.
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{R}_{M1} = \left( \frac{\hbar\Omega - \epsilon_{\text{axial}}}{\hbar\Omega + \epsilon_{\text{axial}}} \right)^2
\end{equation}

\section{VI. Non-Perturbative Euclidean Instanton Action}
The candidate Euclidean action and gap relation are
\begin{equation}
S_E = S_{\text{sym}} + \frac{\pi \epsilon_{\text{axial}}}{\hbar\omega_0} - i \Phi_{\text{top}}, \qquad
\Delta E = 2\sqrt{V_{\text{tunnel}}^2 + \epsilon_{\text{axial}}^2}.
\end{equation}
Their use requires a defined potential, boundary data, and instanton calculation. The suppression and asymptotic claims from the originating proposal remain unproved here.
In particular, the proposed exponential tunneling suppression and the claim that $\Phi_{\text{top}}$ generates odd-even spin staggering are retained as predictions to test, not conclusions of the displayed action alone.
