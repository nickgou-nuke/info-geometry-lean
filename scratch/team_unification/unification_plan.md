# UHF-core GNS equivalence and Cuntz KMS/GNS: separate closure plan

Status: architecture/dependency plan from source inspection, not a proof or build certification. The five prior reports were used only to navigate. I checked the repo-owned declarations they point to; do not treat the staged finite-diagonal candidate as accepted. No source edits or builds were made.

## Boundary and existing infrastructure

These are two related but distinct goals:

**(A) UHF-core representation equivalence.** The concrete finite tower is the `CStarMatrixStage n` tower and normalized trace family (`Canonical/CantorBernoulliCStarMatrixTraceState.lean`); `Canonical/CantorBernoulliGaugeFilteredGNSColimitBridge.lean` already specializes generic filtered GNS to it. It supplies the compatible states, finite-stage GNS, isometric transitions, dense union, and complete Hilbert colimit. Separately, the BitWord matrix carrier has concrete `bitWordMatrixStarRepresentation` into bounded operators on `L2Boundary` and refinement along `dyadicMatrixEmbedding` (`OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliFiniteMatrixStarRepresentationBridge.lean`, `...FiniteMatrixRepresentationRefinementBridge.lean`). The finite BitWord/Fin reindexing is a stagewise `StarAlgEquiv` (`...FiniteMatrixStageTransportBridge.lean`).

The indispensable missing connection is the **transported native tower-bond square**: prove, with all carrier conversions explicit, that the BitWord `dyadicMatrixEmbedding` is carried by the stage equivalences to `cstarConcreteStep` (or corresponding `cstarConcreteMap`). Existing BitWord refinement does not prove that square. Once proved, define the stage maps from the native tower through the equivalences and into `BoundedL2Operator`; derive cocone coherence. Then construct the actual appropriate algebraic/topological C*-colimit morphism, with C*-norm continuity/contractivity and star-algebra laws, rather than inferring a completed representation from finite maps.

To identify Hilbert representations, use the generic maps that already exist (`Canonical/FilteredGNSRepresentation.lean`: state-restriction GNS isometry/intertwining; filtered transition maps; `FilteredGNSColimitRepresentation.lean`: descent from an algebraic `ModuleCat` colimit under an actual target algebra/cocone/state). They do not automatically give a unitary or even an isometric map from this concrete Hilbert colimit to the proposed spatial realization. Specify source/target states and prove the full state square on every stage observable; use it to define the orbit/pre-GNS map, check null-space well-definedness and inner products, show transition compatibility, extend by completion, and prove dense/surjective range (or exhibit inverse). The staged finite-diagonal candidate is only a stagewise diagonal construction per QA report; without a verified transition-natural square and induced colimit map it is not a colimit bridge and is **not accepted**. Cylinder/projection agreement alone cannot yield the full state square.

Word-core estimates are not a substitute: `CantorBernoulliLinearGaugeUHFFactorizationBridge.lean` bounds `phi0`/`tau0` in the finite-support `wordCoreL1Norm`, not the C*-norm. They do not establish bounded extension to a C*-completion, positivity/order properties there, or an infinite UHF state. Prove C*-norm bounds (or use a correctly typed C*-completion theorem) before extending any such functional/map; reconcile its restriction with the compatible finite trace family.

**(B) Full Cuntz KMS/GNS.** This cannot be inferred from (A), nor from the existing generic `CuntzGNSFilteredSystem`/`UHFCuntzGNSColimit` signatures. Those need an actual `CuntzStarTower` and compatible state; finite matrix stages are not such a tower (and finite-matrix Cuntz-2 isometries are obstructed). Word-kernel, diagonal, and equal-depth KMS identities, algebraic quotient dynamics, and spatial vacuum projector calculations have restricted carriers/conclusions. They are not a normalized KMS state on a completed Cuntz algebra.

First name the Cuntz C*-algebra/completion and its universal/canonical maps; provide a compatible Cuntz tower or another rigorous presentation and prove the norm-continuous representation into the chosen target. Define the gauge/time dynamics on that same algebra, with the real-time group law, *-automorphism property, and sign/β convention. Construct a normalized positive functional on a dense analytic *-subalgebra and prove the KMS boundary identity using actual Cuntz multiplication/prefix cancellation; extend by continuity to the completed algebra. Then build its GNS representation and prove the desired relation to any UHF fixed-point/core representation, including state preservation and the needed dense-range/unitary claim. Only after that may a modular/Tomita conclusion be sought, with cyclic/separating hypotheses and operator-level construction proved.

Keep **spatial versus gauge** explicitly separate throughout. The spatial state is on the concrete bounded/generated operator carrier; its proved agreement with the gauge word state is on cylinder projections only. Existing source reports also note a spatial nonfaithfulness witness versus faithful finite matrix trace states. Do not identify their off-diagonal values, states, or GNS representations without a carrier map and a dense-generator state-equality proof. The UHF core trace/GNS construction is not the spatial Cuntz GNS construction.

## Ordered dependency plan

1. **UHF bridge prerequisite:** prove the transported native bond square between `bitWordStageStarAlgEquiv`, `dyadicMatrixEmbedding`, and `cstarConcreteStep`, including exact Mathlib CStar-matrix conversions. Derive stage cocone equality using the existing BitWord `StarAlgHom` and refinement theorem.
2. **UHF completed representation:** define the C*-continuous cocone and induced colimit representation; establish norm control and stage formulas. Do not use the word-core ℓ¹ estimate as C*-norm control.
3. **UHF GNS comparison:** identify the target state pullbacks on all stage elements with `cstarMatrixTraceState`; map GNS classes using the state square; prove isometry, transition compatibility, extension, intertwining, and dense range/surjectivity. Only then state a unitary equivalence. If target is the spatial L² representation, its state must match on all generators, not just diagonal projections.
4. **Cuntz input independently:** give the actual completed Cuntz carrier, tower/representation and norm-continuous maps; establish normalized positive state extension and gauge dynamics on it.
5. **Cuntz KMS then GNS:** prove full analytic-core KMS identity and continuity extension; form GNS; only then compare its fixed-point/UHF-core restriction or representation with (A). Add operator modular data last.
