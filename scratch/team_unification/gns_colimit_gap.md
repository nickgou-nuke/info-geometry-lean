# GNS transport and Cantor–Bernoulli colimit audit

Scope: source inspection only; no owner edits or builds. The working tree was clean at inspection (`git status --short` produced no entries). Claims below are scoped to the cited declarations and searched consumers, not global absence claims.

## Generic transport already proved

`lean/InfoGeometry/Canonical/FilteredGNSRepresentation.lean`, namespace `CStarStateColimit.Native.FilteredGNS`, gives substantial generic transport:

- `restricted_star_mul` / `restricted_star_square`: the GNS sesquilinear/quadratic forms agree after restricting a target `State B` along `f : A →⋆ₐ[ℂ] B`.
- `preGNSMap_inner` and `preGNSLinearIsometry`, followed by `gnsMap` / `gnsMap_isometry`: a complex-linear isometry from the completed GNS space of the restricted state into the target GNS space. It is generally an embedding, not onto.
- `gnsMap_intertwines_toPreGNS` and `gnsMap_intertwines`: intertwining for the source action and target action precomposed by `f`, on the dense algebraic vectors and then the whole completion. `gnsMap_comp_toPreGNS` records composition on generators.
- For a compatible filtered family, `filteredGNSMap_*` proves isometric transitions, dense-subspace and completed-space intertwining, identity and composition. `gnsDirectInductiveSystem` packages the coherent maps; `gnsColimitInclusion_transition` and `gnsColimitInclusion_representation_compat` give colimit compatibility.

This is generic, state-restriction-based GNS functoriality. No surjectivity/unitary conclusion follows for arbitrary `f`: its image need not generate all target GNS vectors.

`Canonical/FilteredGNSHilbertColimit.lean` builds `RealDirectLimit` and its Hilbert completion (`AlgebraicGNSHilbertColimit`, `GNSHilbertColimit`), stage isometries, transition compatibility, density of the union of stage images, and completeness. This is a Hilbert-space colimit, not itself an algebra colimit.

`Canonical/FilteredGNSColimitRepresentation.lean` takes a genuine `StarInductiveCocone` into an already supplied C⋆-algebra `Ainf` and a state `Ω`; `restrictedStateFamily`, `stageTargetGNSMap`, and `descendGNSColimit` give a linear map from the categorical `ModuleCat` colimit into `Ω.functional.GNS`. `descendGNSColimit_stage` and `descendGNSColimit_representation` establish its stage restrictions and representation compatibility. The module header expressly disclaims a C⋆-algebra colimit category. No surjectivity or isometry of this universal map is asserted there.

## Exact sufficient conditions for a state-preserving unitary equivalence

For two unital C⋆-algebras `A,B`, normalized positive states `φ,ψ`, and a unital star-algebra isomorphism `e : A ≃⋆ₐ[ℂ] B`, it is sufficient that `ψ (e a) = φ a` for every `a : A`. On pre-GNS classes define `[a] ↦ [e a]`. The state identity gives preservation of inner products since `ψ ((e a)†(e b)) = φ (a†b)`; it also makes the map well-defined on null quotients. Its inverse is induced by `e.symm`, so the completed extension is a surjective complex-linear isometry, hence a unitary equivalence. Intertwining is `U (πφ(a)x) = πψ(e a)(U x)`, first on classes and then by continuity. For an algebra homomorphism rather than an isomorphism, the existing `gnsMap` only becomes onto if the target GNS space is the closure of the image-generated orbit (equivalently, the isometric image has dense range); an explicit inverse or dense-range proof is required.

For two representations `π_A,π_B` on Hilbert spaces with unit cyclic vectors `ξ,η`, another sufficient package is a unital star-isomorphism `e`, equality of vector states `⟪ξ,π_A(a)ξ⟫ = ⟪η,π_B(e a)η⟫` for all `a`, and cyclicity of both vectors. The map on orbit vectors `π_A(a)ξ ↦ π_B(e a)η` preserves inner products by the state equality, extends by density to a unitary, sends `ξ` to `η`, and intertwines. Without cyclicity it canonically identifies only the cyclic subrepresentations.

## Gap statement, carefully scoped

The inspected concrete finite matrix bridge proves finite trace/GNS facts plus a native Hilbert colimit, while the generic target descent requires an actual C⋆-algebra target/cocone and state. The inspected spatial bridge proves cyclicity for its own spatial state, and the modular bridge is scalar/index-level. The searched declarations do not provide, in these files, the state-preserving unitary equivalence identifying the finite-matrix gauge GNS colimit with a specified full Cuntz/UHF C⋆-algebra GNS representation, nor a proof that a relevant descent map is onto. Nor do these cited owners establish a completed infinite C⋆-colimit/KMS-GNS identification or an operator-level Tomita/KMS bridge for that identification. This is a bounded finding from the inspected owners/search; other generic Cuntz, completion, or modular declarations must be assessed by their exact hypotheses and conclusions before a repository-wide absence claim.

A concrete bridge would need: (1) a named target C⋆ algebra and actual universal/canonical cocone from the matrix tower, with isometric/norm-compatible legs and coherence; (2) a normalized positive state on that target whose pullbacks are exactly `cstarMatrixTraceState n` (not merely matching selected matrix units); (3) a map from the Hilbert colimit into that target-state GNS, using `descendGNSColimit` or the completion analogue, and proof of isometry; (4) dense range/surjectivity, e.g. target cyclicity plus density of the union of cocone-leg images, or a source/target state-preserving star-isomorphism; (5) generator-level compatibility identifying matrix-stage embeddings with the concrete Cuntz/UHF generators; and for modular/KMS claims, a proved KMS state hypothesis and operator-level modular/Tomita construction and compatibility. Finite faithfulness, finite vacuum expectation, and scalar modular coefficients alone do not supply these premises.
