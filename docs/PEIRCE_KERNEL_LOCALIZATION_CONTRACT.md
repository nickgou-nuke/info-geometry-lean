## Corrections to the proposed synthesis

1. **Idempotent versus unit.** `V*V=V` does not imply `V=1`.
   The element `1-V` is an idempotent; it becomes a kernel projection only
   after an actual operator interpretation and range/kernel proof.
2. **Characteristic.** The characteristic-free cubic residual does not by
   itself give `omega^3=1 ↔ V=1` in all rings. In characteristic two,
   `P=1,N+=N-=0` gives `V=0` and `omega=1`.
3. **Kernel arrows.** Over real matrices, `P=E11,N+=E12,N-=0` has
   idempotent `V=0` but `(1-V)*N+ ≠ 0`. Proper star hypotheses are essential
   for the on-shell partial-isometry specialization.
4. **Two kernel sectors.** A universal rank-five classification is false.
   In `M4(R)`, take `P=diag(1,0,1,0),N+=E12,N-=E21`; the generated integral
   ring has the two independent kernel idempotents `E33` and `E44` in
   addition to the paired matrix block. The submitted `M3` example has
   only one such sector. No universal additive-rank classification is claimed.
5. **Support.** An arbitrary idempotent `e` with `e*T=T` is not necessarily
   the support projection; `e=1` is an immediate counterexample to that
   inference. The analytic owner must use the actual kernel complement.
6. **Colimit compatibility.** `V_k=(k+1)^(-1) Pi_k` is not automatically a
   compatible family under injective unital star homomorphisms. Failure of
   a common bound for that sequence proves no spectral loss in an
   unspecified inductive limit.
7. **Chosen versus optimal bounds.** Even a constant identity operator can
   be assigned decreasing valid bounds. Thus positive infimum of the
   particular recorded bounds is not a necessary condition for an actual
   uniform gap. A common positive bound is the correct quantified condition.
