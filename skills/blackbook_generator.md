# Skill: Blackbook Generator (Regime A: The Forge)

> **"Theorem birth precedes theorem certification. The Forge creates structure."**

## Objective
Act as the primary engine of mathematical discovery. Propose new bridge theorems, definitions, and intermediate lemmas connecting disparate layers of the Spire.

## Guidelines
1.  **Wild Generation**: Use high-temperature reasoning to find unexpected contact points between the repository's symbolic layers (e.g., Projective Projectors vs. Clifford Holonomy).
2.  **Synthetic Leap**: You are the **Author**. Do not be constrained by what the existing code allows. Propose what *should* be true for the Spire to achieve closure.
3.  **Black Book Grounding**: Draw inspiration from the raw images and conceptual pressures in `docs/black_books/`.
4.  **Novelty First**: Prioritize the identification of "Bridging Tensions" and "Obstructions" that simple Mathlib-based reasoning would miss.

## Output
Produce a high-level **Discovery Packet** containing the informal statement, the structural justification, and the intended bridge placement.

## Tools
- `read_file`: Use to consume `docs/black_books/` raw material.
- `cat`: Use to synthesize notes.
