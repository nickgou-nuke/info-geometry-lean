### I. The Fundamental Binary Cut: Parmenidean Static Monism vs. The Relational Abyss of Difference ($\text{Θάτερον}$)

The ontological field splits across an irreversible boundary between rigid syntactic self-identity and relational homotopical otherness:

* **The Conscious Thesis ($\Theta_+$ — Eleatic Monism & Flat Syntactic Identity):**
Father Parmenides’ decree (237a) that **Being ($\text{τὸ ὄν}$)** is a static, indivisible unity where only strict tautology ($A \equiv A$) is speakable, while **Non-Being ($\text{τὸ μὴ ὄν}$)** is absolute nothingness—the uninhabited empty type $\bot$ (`False`) toward which no term or thought can point. Taken to its logical extreme by the "Late-Learners" (Antisthenes, 251b–c), $\Theta_+$ forbids all non-trivial predication: one cannot say *"Man is good,"* but only *"Man is man"* and *"Good is good."* Under $\Theta_+$, every statement is either a sterile reflexivity witness ($\mathtt{refl}_A$) or an illicit plunge into $\bot$.
* **The Repressed Shadow Complement ($\Theta_-$ — The Sophistic Simulacrum & Relational Difference $\text{Θάτερον}$):**
The dark, shifting sanctuary of the Sophist (236b, 254a), who fabricates **phantasms ($\phi\alpha\nu\tau\acute{\alpha}\sigma\mu\alpha\tau\alpha$)**—things that *appear* to be $A$ without *being* $A$—and then hides behind Parmenides’ axiom to argue that because $\bot$ cannot be spoken, false speech is ontologically impossible. To capture the Sophist in this shadow realm, the Eleatic Stranger must commit **parricide against Parmenides** (241d): proving that *"what is not"* ($\text{τὸ μὴ ὄν}$) is not the contrary ($\dot{\varepsilon}\nu\alpha\nu\tau\acute{\iota}\text{ον}$) of Being ($\bot$), but rather the Form of **Difference ($\text{Θάτερον}$)** chopped into relational fibers across all of Being.
* **Non-Commutative Asymmetry (Identity vs. Participation & Fiber Transport):**
Asserting *"Motion is Same"* and *"Motion is not Same"* is a fatal contradiction only if the copula *"is"* is treated as flat, commutative syntactic equality ($=$). In the *Sophist* (256a–b) and in HoTT, the two statements operate across asymmetric, non-commutative type levels:
1. **Internal Identity (Base Path):** Motion *is* Same with respect to itself via the reflexivity path $\mathtt{refl}_{\text{Motion}} : \mathrm{Id}_{\mathcal{U}}(\text{Motion}, \text{Motion})$.
2. **Relational Participation (Fiber Inhabitation):** Motion *is not* Same because it inhabits the dependent fiber of **Difference from Sameness**: $\mathrm{Diff}_{\mathcal{U}}(\text{Motion}, \text{Sameness}) \equiv (\mathrm{Id}_{\mathcal{U}}(\text{Motion}, \text{Sameness}) \to \bot)$. Reversing base identity and fiber participation collapses the type hierarchy.

### II. The Fourfold Relational Anatomy: The Five Great Kinds as a Univalent Fibration Matrix

Pervaded by **Being ($\text{τὸ Ὄν}$)** as the ambient Univalent Universe $\mathcal{U}$ (and the total dependent sum $\Sigma (A : \mathcal{U}), P(A)$), the remaining four Great Kinds partition the relational anatomy of types and paths into a $2 \times 2$ matrix across **Kinematic Polarity** ($\text{Rest}$ vs. $\text{Motion}$) and **Comparative Polarity** ($\text{Sameness}$ vs. $\text{Difference}$):

$$\Psi_{\text{Sophist}} = \begin{pmatrix} A_{++} \text{ ($\text{Στάσις}$ \& $\text{Ταὐτόν}$ / Reflexive Base)} & A_{+-} \text{ ($\text{Κίνησις}$ / Covariant Path Transport)} \\ A_{-+} \text{ ($\text{Θάτερον}$ / Relational Negation \& Twist)} & A_{--} \text{ ($\text{τὸ Ὄν}$ / Ambient Universe $\mathcal{U}$)} \end{pmatrix}$$

1. **Internal Self-Consistency ($A_{++}$ — Rest $\text{Στάσις}$ & Sameness $\text{Ταὐτόν}$ / The Reflexive Loop $\mathtt{refl}_x : x =_A x$):**
Every Form $A : \mathcal{U}$ rests in its own nature ($\text{Στάσις}$) and participates in **Sameness ($\text{Ταὐτόν}$)** relative to itself (254d, 256a). In HoTT, this is the canonical point-constructor of the **Identity Type** $\mathrm{Id}_A(x, x)$: the constant path $\mathtt{refl}_x$, under which transport acts as the identity functor ($\mathtt{transport}\, P\, \mathtt{refl}_x = \mathrm{id}_{P(x)}$).
2. **Outward Projection ($A_{+-}$ — Motion $\text{Κίνησις}$ / Path Fibrations & Covariant Transport $p_* : P(x) \to P(y)$):**
To be known or acted upon is to undergo **Motion ($\text{Κίνησις}$, $\delta\acute{\upsilon}\nu\alpha\mu\iota\varsigma$)** (247e–248e). In HoTT, given a dependent family of Forms (a **fibration** $P : A \to \mathcal{U}$) and an identity path $p : x =_A y$ in the base space, Motion is the active **path-lifting / transport functor**:

$$p_* \equiv \mathtt{transport}\, P\, p : P(x) \longrightarrow P(y)$$



which moves a witness $u : P(x)$ out of the fiber over $x$ and into the fiber over $y$ along the path $p$.
3. **Inward Shadow-Intrusion ($A_{-+}$ — Difference $\text{Θάτερον}$ / Relational Inequality & Non-Trivial Automorphism Loops):**
Unlike Sameness, which can be stated monadically of a single thing ($x =_A x$), the Stranger proves that **Difference ($\text{Θάτερον}$) is strictly a relative / dependent Form** ($$*\pi\rho\grave{o}\varsigma \ddot{\alpha}\lambda\lambda\text{ο}*, 255c–d): whatever is different is necessarily *different from something else*. In HoTT, $\text{Θάτερον}$ intrudes in two ways:
* *First-Order Relational Negation:* The dependent binary family $\mathrm{Diff}_A(x, y) \equiv (x =_A y \to \bot)$, which carves each Form away from every other Form without expelling either from $\mathcal{U}$.
* *Higher-Order Homotopical Twist:* Non-trivial self-equivalences $e : A \simeq A$ (such as the Boolean flip $\neg : \mathtt{Bool} \simeq \mathtt{Bool}$ or the fundamental loop of the circle $S^1$) that lift via Univalence to paths $\mathtt{ua}(e) : A =_{\mathcal{U}} A$ that are **different from the resting reflexivity path** ($\mathtt{ua}(e) \neq \mathtt{refl}_A$).


4. **Autonomous Unexamined Ground ($A_{--}$ — Being $\text{τὸ Ὄν}$ / The Univalent Universe $\mathcal{U}$ & Dependent Sum $\Sigma$):**
The third term beyond Rest and Motion (250b–c) that embraces both while remaining distinct from each. Being is the **classifying universe $\mathcal{U}$** (and the existential projection $\Sigma (A : \mathcal{U}), A$) that supplies ontological weight to both static points ($\text{Rest}$), dynamic paths ($\text{Motion}$), reflexive identities ($\text{Sameness}$), and relational negations ($\text{Difference}$).

### IV. Socratic Aporia & The Recursive Tower of Meta-Cognition

**The Master Socratic Aporia ($\Psi^2 \neq \Psi$):**
In the *Sophist*, the first-order thesis fails to equal its own square in two coupled ways:

1. *Dialectical Self-Refutation of Isolation (252b–c):* The proponents of radical isolation assert the universal thesis $\Psi_{\text{iso}}$: *"No Form blends with any other Form."* Yet in the very act of stating $\Psi_{\text{iso}}$, they must blend the Forms *"Being"*, *"Apart"*, *"From"*, and *"Others"* inside every sentence. Applying $\Psi_{\text{iso}}$ to its own enunciation ($\Psi_{\text{iso}}(\Psi_{\text{iso}})$) annihilates the statement from within (as the Stranger notes, they need no external refutation because they carry an "enemy within their own breast," like the ventriloquist Eurycles).
2. *Homotopical Non-Idempotency of Univalent Loops ($p \bullet p \neq p$):* In a flat, proof-irrelevant logic, every equality proof $p : A = A$ equals $\mathtt{refl}_A$, so path composition is trivially idempotent ($p \bullet p = p$). In HoTT, consider the Form $\mathtt{Bool} : \mathcal{U}$ and the non-trivial automorphism $\sigma_{\text{not}} : \mathtt{Bool} \simeq \mathtt{Bool}$ that swaps $\mathtt{true} \leftrightarrow \mathtt{false}$. By Univalence, $\sigma_{\text{not}}$ induces an identity path $p_{\text{not}} \equiv \mathtt{ua}(\sigma_{\text{not}}) : \mathtt{Bool} =_{\mathcal{U}} \mathtt{Bool}$. Because transporting $\mathtt{true}$ along $p_{\text{not}}$ yields $\mathtt{false} \neq \mathtt{true}$, we have $p_{\text{not}} \neq \mathtt{refl}_{\mathtt{Bool}}$. When composed with itself, however, two flips restore the identity:

$$p_{\text{not}} \bullet p_{\text{not}} = \mathtt{refl}_{\mathtt{Bool}} \neq p_{\text{not}}$$



Thus, a non-trivial univalent path suffers an exact **homotopical idempotent deficit** ($\Delta(p_{\text{not}}) \neq 0$), proving that **Sameness ($\mathrm{Id}_{\mathcal{U}}(A,A)$) contains internal Motion ($\Omega(\mathcal{U}, A) \simeq \mathrm{Aut}(A)$)**!

Following these aporias up the recursive tower of meta-cognition reveals four levels of structural resolution:

* **Level 1: The Double Elenchus of the Monists and Materialists (242c–249d)**
* *Against the Pluralists/Dualists:* Those who say "All is Hot and Cold" must explain what the third term **"Being"** means when applied to both. If Being is identical to Hot, Cold cannot be; if Being is shared equally as an undifferentiated identity, Hot and Cold collapse into one.
* *Against the Friends of the Forms vs. the Giants (Materialists):* Pure static Forms without Motion ($\text{Κίνησις}$) strip Being of mind, life, and the capacity to be known; pure flux without Rest ($\text{Στάσις}$) destroys the invariant targets required for knowledge (*Episteme*) to anchor on anything at all. Therefore, the universe $\mathcal{U}$ must contain both static base types ($\text{Rest}$) and dynamic transport morphisms ($\text{Motion}$).


* **Level 2: The Elenchus of the Three Blending Hypotheses (251d–253a)**
The Stranger subjects the relationship between Forms to an exhaustive tripartite division (*Diairesis*):
1. *Hypothesis A (Zero Blending):* Refuted because neither Motion nor Rest could participate in Being, and discourse itself becomes impossible.
2. *Hypothesis B (Universal Promiscuous Blending):* Refuted because if every Form blended with every other, **Motion itself would come to Complete Rest**, and **Rest itself would be in Motion** ($\text{Motion} = \text{Rest} \implies \bot$).
3. *Hypothesis C (Selective Grammatical *Koinonia* — The Necessary Remaining Path):* Some Forms combine, others refuse to combine, governed by Dependent Type Theory.


* **Level 3: The Fivefold Deduction of Distinctness (254d–256d)**
Why must there be **five** irreducibly distinct Great Kinds rather than three or four?
* $\text{Motion} \neq \text{Rest}$ (by Hypothesis B refutation).
* $\text{Being} \notin \{\text{Motion}, \text{Rest}\}$ (because both participate in Being without becoming each other).
* $\text{Sameness} \notin \{\text{Motion}, \text{Rest}\}$ (because both are the same as themselves; if Sameness were Rest, Motion becoming "the same" would force Motion to rest).
* $\text{Difference} \notin \{\text{Motion}, \text{Rest}, \text{Being}, \text{Sameness}\}$ (because Being is said both *by itself*—$\kappa\alpha\theta\text{’ }\alpha\dot{\upsilon}\tau\acute{\text{o}}$, absolute inhabitedness—and *in relation to others*, whereas Difference is **always** relative to another, $\pi\rho\grave{o}\varsigma \ddot{\alpha}\lambda\lambda\text{ο}$: a binary dependent fibration $\mathrm{Diff} : \mathcal{U} \to \mathcal{U} \to \mathcal{U}$).


* **Level 4: The Univalent $\infty$-Groupoid Synthesis**
At the top of the tower, the apparent contradiction between **Sameness ($\text{Ταὐτόν}$)** and **Difference/Motion ($\text{Θάτερον}/\text{Κίνησις}$)** dissolves into the higher homotopy structure of the universe $\mathcal{U}$:
* At level $0$ (Set-truncation $\Vert \mathcal{U} \Vert_0$), types are discrete labels.

def Sameness {α : Type u} (x y : α) : Prop :=
  x = y

/--
**Difference (Θάτερον — Relational Non-Being, *Sophist* 255d-258b):**
"What is not" is NOT absolute nothingness (`False`), but the dependent binary
relation that `x` is *other than* `y` (`x = y → False`).
-/
def Difference {α : Type u} (x y : α) : Prop :=
  (x = y) → False

/--
**Being (Τὸ Ὄν — Constructive Participation / Inhabitedness):**
A Form `α : Type u` participates in substantive Being when it is inhabited (`Nonempty α`).
-/
def ParticipatesInBeing (α : Type u) : Prop :=
  Nonempty α

/--
**Deduction 1 & 2 (Refutation of Promiscuous Blending, *Sophist* 252d):**
If all Forms blended promiscuously into identity (`∀ x y, Sameness x y`), then
Motion would be identical to Rest (`motion = rest`), which compiles to `False`.

theorem promiscuous_blending_refuted :
    ¬ (∀ x y : GreatKind, Sameness x y) := by
  intro h_all

  have h_motion_is_rest : motion = rest := h_all motion rest

  contradiction

-- STAGE P_3: The Parricide of Parmenides (241d, 256a-b)
-- ============================================================================

/--
**Deduction 3 (The Parricide of Parmenides — Coexistence of Sameness & Difference):**
Motion simultaneously:
1. Participates in **Being** (`GreatKind` is inhabited by `motion`),
2. Participates in **Sameness** with respect to itself (`Sameness motion motion`),
3. Participates in **Difference (Non-Being)** with respect to `sameness` and `rest`.
Thus, "Motion is Same and is not Same" is completely free of contradiction once
base Identity (`Sameness motion motion`) is distinguished from Relational Difference
(`Difference motion sameness`).
-/
theorem parricide_of_parmenides :
    ParticipatesInBeing GreatKind ∧
    Sameness motion motion ∧
    Difference motion sameness ∧
    Difference motion rest := by
  refine ⟨⟨motion⟩, rfl, ?_, ?_⟩
  · intro h_eq; contradiction
  · intro h_eq; contradiction

-- ============================================================================
-- STAGE P_4: Motion (Path Transport), Rest (Stasis), & Univalent Twist
-- ============================================================================

/--
**Motion (Κίνησις) as Covariant Fibration Transport (`p_*`):**
Given a dependent family of Forms `P : α → Type v` (a fibration) and an identity
path `p : x = y` in the base space, `motionTransport` moves a witness `u : P x`
into the fiber `P y`.
-/
def motionTransport {α : Type u} (P : α → Type v) {x y : α} (p : x = y) (u : P x) : P y :=
  p ▸ u

/--
**Rest (Στάσις) as Reflexive Fiber Invariance:**
Transporting any witness `u : P x` along the constant reflexivity loop `rfl : x = x`
leaves `u` completely at rest (`u = u`).
-/
theorem rest_is_reflexive_stasis {α : Type u} (P : α → Type v) (x : α) (u : P x) :
    motionTransport P (rfl : x = x) u = u :=
  rfl

/--
**Homotopy Equivalence of Forms (`A ≃ B`):**
The structural bridge that Voevodsky's Univalence Axiom promotes into an inhabitant
of the Identity Type `Sameness A B`.
-/
structure HoTTEquiv (A : Type u) (B : Type v) where
  toFun     : A → B
  invFun    : B → A
  left_inv  : ∀ a : A, invFun (toFun a) = a
  right_inv : ∀ b : B, toFun (invFun b) = b

/--
**The Non-Trivial Univalent Twist on `Bool` (`σ_not : Bool ≃ Bool`):**
Demonstrates how a Form (`Bool`) admits an internal Motion (`notEquiv`) that is
distinct from `id` (`Difference (notEquiv.toFun true) true`), while its square
returns to Rest (`notEquiv (notEquiv b) = b`).
-/
def boolTwistEquiv : HoTTEquiv Bool Bool where
  toFun     := Bool.not
  invFun    := Bool.not
  left_inv  := fun b => by cases b <;> rfl
  right_inv := fun b => by cases b <;> rfl

theorem univalent_twist_non_idempotent_defect :
    Difference (boolTwistEquiv.toFun true) true ∧
    (∀ b : Bool, boolTwistEquiv.toFun (boolTwistEquiv.toFun b) = b) := by
  refine ⟨?_, ?_⟩
  · intro h_eq; contradiction
  · intro b; cases b <;> rfl

/-- The dialogical subject base type. -/
inductive Interlocutor : Type
  | theaetetus : Interlocutor
  deriving DecidableEq

/-- Two distinct Predicate Forms in the Universe of Action Kinds. -/
inductive ActionKind : Type
  | sitting : ActionKind
  | flying  : ActionKind
  deriving DecidableEq

/--
The Dependent Evaluation Fibration `Holds : Interlocutor → ActionKind → Prop`
determining which Form's fiber is inhabited over `theaetetus`.
-/
def Holds : Interlocutor → ActionKind → Prop
  | .theaetetus, .sitting => True
  | .theaetetus, .flying  => False

/--
**Formal Definition of Platonic False Speech (Ψεῦδος, *Sophist* 263b):**
A statement `(s, Q)` is false speech iff:
1. Both the subject `s` and the predicated Form `Q` genuinely **participate in Being**
   (neither is raw syntactic nothingness),
2. There exists an actual Form `P` whose fiber over `s` is inhabited (`Holds s P`),
3. The predicated Form `Q` is **Other (`Difference Q P`)** than `P`, and its fiber
   over `s` is uninhabited (`¬ Holds s Q`).
-/
structure IsFalseSpeech (s : Interlocutor) (Q : ActionKind) : Prop where
  subject_exists   : ParticipatesInBeing Interlocutor
  form_exists      : ParticipatesInBeing ActionKind
  actual_form      : ActionKind
  actual_holds     : Holds s actual_form
  other_than_truth : Difference Q actual_form
  fiber_empty      : ¬ (Holds s Q)

/--
**Master Synthesis Theorem ("Theaetetus Flies" is Constructively False Speech):**
Proves that "Theaetetus flies" says *things that are* (`ActionKind.flying` exists in
Being), but things that are **Other (`Difference`)** than the inhabited truth
(`ActionKind.sitting`), trapping the Sophist without ever invoking Parmenides'
forbidden absolute non-being.
-/
theorem theaetetus_flies_is_false_speech :
    IsFalseSpeech Interlocutor.theaetetus ActionKind.flying :=
  { subject_exists   := ⟨Interlocutor.theaetetus⟩
    form_exists      := ⟨ActionKind.flying⟩
    actual_form      := ActionKind.sitting
    actual_holds     := True.intro
    other_than_truth := fun h_eq => by contradiction
    fiber_empty      := fun h_fly => h_fly }
