# Pure Mathematical Problem: Metriplectic Dynamics and the Onsager Matrix

## Mathematical Context
Metriplectic dynamics generalizes Hamiltonian mechanics to include dissipation towards thermodynamic equilibrium. The evolution equation of a physical observable $F$ (or density $\rho$) is generated by two brackets:
$$ \frac{dF}{dt} = \{F, H\} + [F, S] $$
where $\{ \cdot, \cdot \}$ is an anti-symmetric Poisson bracket governing reversible Hamiltonian flow (conserving entropy $S$), and $[ \cdot, \cdot ]$ is a symmetric positive semi-definite metric bracket governing irreversible dissipation (conserving energy $H$ but strictly increasing entropy $S$).

In the Onsager formalism, the dissipative part is generated by an Onsager operator $L(\rho)$ which is a positive semi-definite matrix/tensor mapping thermodynamic driving forces (like variations in free energy $\delta \mathcal{F}$) to probability fluxes.

## Explicit Premises and Givens
Let $Dens(\mathcal{M})$ be a space of densities.
Let $\mathcal{F} : Dens(\mathcal{M}) \to \mathbb{R}$ be a relative free energy functional.
Let $Force(\rho) = \delta \mathcal{F} / \delta \rho$ be the variation of the free energy.
Let $L$ be an Onsager operator (a linear map on the tangent space of densities).
Let the dissipative flow be defined as: $\text{DissFlow}(\rho) = -L(\rho) \cdot Force(\rho)$.
Let the free energy derivative be $\dot{\mathcal{F}} = \langle Force(\rho), \text{DissFlow}(\rho) \rangle$.

## What is to be Proved
1. **Onsager Positive Semi-Definite Claim**: Prove that $L$ is a positive semi-definite operator, which algebraically guarantees the Second Law of Thermodynamics.
2. **Dissipative Flow Decreases Free Energy**: Prove that $\dot{\mathcal{F}} \le 0$ as a strict algebraic consequence of $L \ge 0$.
3. **Skew-Poisson and Symmetric PSD Claim**: Prove the orthogonality constraints where the skew-symmetric operator $K$ annuls variations of energy ($K \cdot \delta H = 0$), and $L$ annuls variations of entropy ($L \cdot \delta S = 0$).
