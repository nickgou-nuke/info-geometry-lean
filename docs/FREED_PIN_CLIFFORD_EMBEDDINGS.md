# Ordered Clifford embeddings for Freed's Pin construction

## Source and theorem contract

This intake is Daniel S. Freed, *Index Theory on Pin Manifolds*,
arXiv:2405.20464v2 (25 July 2024), supplied as `2405.20464v2.pdf`.
The source PDF has SHA-256
`9e38a69c506cfa2d29a77cead45d282f754d859071562c90acea4f8b70265dca`.
The implementation baseline is upstream commit
`a392af178af1fe2bcba2ba39eb405700c6ee103c`, independently of PR #336.
Lean `v4.34.1` and Mathlib
`d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612` remain pinned.

The selected mathematical claim is the ordered Clifford-algebra construction
behind equations (1.9)-(1.10), on page 3. The source generator maps to `v * e`,
where the added orthogonal generator has the opposite square. The carrier is
the actual universal `CliffordAlgebra`, with its native even subalgebra, over
a commutative scalar ring and an arbitrary module. The construction retains
the Clifford multiplication and its order.

| Source claim | Formal target and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Positive-square Pin generators map into a Clifford algebra with one negative direction | Construct `Cl(Q) ≃ₐ[R] even Cl(Q ⊕ -sq)`, with generator image exactly `ι(v,0) * ι(0,1)`. |
| Negative-square Pin generators map into a Clifford algebra with one positive direction | Construct `Cl(-Q) ≃ₐ[R] even Cl(Q ⊕ sq)`, with the same ordered generator image. |
| These maps are embeddings | Prove injectivity of the ambient algebra maps from actual inverse equivalences. |
| Pin groups are generated by normalized vectors | Define the subgroup of Clifford units generated by vectors with quadratic value `1` or `-1`; prove both maps restrict to injective group homomorphisms into the even normalized-vector subgroup. |
| The target is Spin | Use the explicitly defined even subgroup. Identification with a particular connected Lie group is outside this contract. |
| Opposite Clifford shifts give KO-degree shifts | The algebraic embeddings are inputs to that argument. Graded Morita equivalence, Dirac operators on manifolds, Fredholm theory, KO pushforwards, and eta invariants are separate obligations. |

## Existing owners and convention checks

Native Mathlib `LinearAlgebra/CliffordAlgebra/EvenEquiv.lean` already proves
`CliffordAlgebra.equivEven Q` and `evenEquivEvenNeg Q`. The first sends a
generator to `e * v`, opposite to the order in Freed's (1.10). Composing with
the native Clifford involution supplies the positive branch's required sign.
For the negative branch the native even-algebra sign-change equivalence
supplies the sign. Both constructions must be proved on generators, rather
than inferred from the names of the equivalences.

`Clifford/Cl55RealSplitPin.lean` supplies an existing, dimension-specific
normalized-vector subgroup definition. Its `Cl55WittPinSignatureBoundary`
dependency already records why Mathlib's `pinGroup` does not by itself model
the split-signature convention. The new generic owner follows that definition
on an arbitrary quadratic module, without importing the heavy `(5,5)` owner.

Mathlib's Clifford star is reverse composed with involution. Thus a vector
has star norm `-Q(v)`, and a positive-square normalized generator does not
lie in its unitary `pinGroup` over the reals. A suspended positive generator
also has star norm `-1`, so it cannot be identified with an element of the
native unitary `spinGroup`. The formal target is the explicitly defined even
normalized-vector subgroup.

Equation (1.5) on page 2 prints `Pin ⊂ Cl⁰`, although the immediately
preceding definition includes odd vector generators. The formalization does
not use that inclusion. Even Clifford units and an identity component of a
real Lie group are also distinct targets. No connected-component or
topological-covering identification is asserted.

The owner search covers repository filenames and declaration contents for
Pin, Spin, Clifford suspension, reflection, and Dirac constructions; the
related normalized-vector and signature-boundary owners; and the pinned
Mathlib even-algebra, conjugation, inversion, and group APIs. These are
bounded source searches, not a claim that every historical owner compiles.

## Exact implementation scope

`Clifford/FreedCliffordSuspension.lean` constructs the two ordered algebra
equivalences, their ambient injective maps, and their exact ranges. It proves
inverse formulas on arbitrary even vector pairs, ordered pair-product laws,
the opposite squares of the added directions, and the identity which turns
source Clifford conjugation into target reversion. No choice of basis or
invertibility of two is required.

`Clifford/NormalizedVectorPin.lean` constructs normalized vector units, their
generated subgroup, and the even subgroup as a comap along the native
even-unit inclusion. It proves that each suspended source generator is a
product of two normalized target vectors. Subgroup closure constructs both
restricted homomorphisms, and the algebra equivalences prove their
injectivity. Surjectivity onto the entire even Pin subgroup is not asserted.

The two algebraic maps explain the sign mechanism in the first section of
the paper. They do not construct principal bundles, smooth equivariant
spinor fields, lifted Levi-Civita derivatives, geometric Dirac operators,
graded Morita equivalences, Fredholm families, KO groups, or pushforwards.
In particular, the paper explicitly labels its Pin-positive differential
KO/eta comparison **Conjecture 5.10** (page 15). Its conjectural status in
that source is preserved; these algebraic constructions prove no
analytic eta or differential index theorem.

## Dependency order and acceptance

Quadratic forms and native Clifford relations precede the even-algebra
equivalences. Their exact generator formulas precede transport of normalized
vector units. Subgroup closure then constructs the group maps, while
injectivity is inherited from the algebra equivalences. No later geometric
claim is used as a premise for an earlier algebraic result.

Acceptance requires kernel compilation of the focused owners, declaration
axiom audit, source/debt review, and the maintained staged proof gate. The
root publication gate is checked separately. Its pre-existing absent
`HomotopyHypothesis` path dependency must remain visible if it still blocks
root acceptance; no dependency pin or gate is changed for this intake.
