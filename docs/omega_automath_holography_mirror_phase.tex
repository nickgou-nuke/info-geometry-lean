\begin{abstract}
This manuscript records a theorem-safe synthesis of the finite Lean 4 and SymPy
artifacts currently present in the \texttt{info-geometry-lean} repository.  The
verified core is a family of finite algebraic corridors: Cuntz/UHF horizon
nilpotence and reconstruction identities, scalar Bekenstein--Hawking/dyadic
entropy calibrations under explicit nonzero-denominator hypotheses, common-phase
modular-flow crossing invariance, an inductive-colimit crystal readout for the
$Cl(1,1)$ tensor tower, and a finite two-branch attention projection.

The document intentionally does not claim a proof of full AdS/CFT, the Black
Hole Information Paradox, HKLL in its analytic continuum form, the Standard
Model Higgs mechanism, the Riemann Hypothesis, the Mass Gap theorem, or
hallucination-freedom of arbitrary large language models.  Instead, it records
what is kernel-checked: explicit finite identities and conditional bridges that
can serve as stable atoms for future continuum or physical hypotheses.
\end{abstract}

\section{Common-Phase Modular Crossing}

The module \texttt{ModularFlowKMS} isolates a purely algebraic KMS-shaped
calculation.  Suppose two elements transform by the same central unitary phase
under a flow $\sigma_t$.  Then Lean proves
\begin{equation}
  \sigma_t(XY^*) = XY^*.
\end{equation}
The symmetric crossing
\begin{equation}
  S_L S_R^* + S_R S_L^*
\end{equation}
is fixed because both summands are fixed.  The theorem surface is finite and
conditional.  It does not construct an unbounded Tomita operator, prove analytic
KMS boundary conditions, or identify the Standard Model Higgs field.

The finite-to-infinite substrate is formalized in the $Cl(1,1)$ tensor-tower
modules and summarized in \texttt{InductiveColimitCrystal}.  The finite atom at
stage $n$ is denoted \texttt{AtomStage n}.  The algebraic crystal carrier is the

stage advance
\begin{equation}
  A \longmapsto A \otimes I_2,
\end{equation}
abstracted by \texttt{bindAtoms}.

Lean proves that finite binding does not change the represented crystal element:
\begin{equation}
  \iota_{m+k}(\operatorname{bind}_{m,k}(A)) = \iota_m(A).
\end{equation}
It also proves transport of square-zero atoms, idempotent atoms, and inner
commutator readouts, together with stability of normalized Markov trace and
normalized log-determinant readouts.

The SymPy witness \texttt{inductive\_colimit\_crystal.py} checks the concrete

\begin{align}

    \operatorname{tr}_{\mathrm{norm}}(A), \\

and verifies commutator, idempotent, and square-zero transport.

\section{Epoch 4: Mirror-Phase Finite Attention}

The finite attention corridor lives in \texttt{MirrorPhaseCuntzAttention}.  The
exact two-branch attention matrix is
\begin{equation}
  A = \begin{pmatrix}
    \frac12 & \frac12 \\
    \frac12 & \frac12
  \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Lean proves:
\begin{align}
  A^2 &= A, \\
  \sum_j A_{ij} &= 1, \\
  A_{ij} &\ge 0.
\end{align}
For a two-branch vector $v=(v_0,v_1)$, applying attention gives
\begin{equation}
  Av = \left(\frac{v_0+v_1}{2},\frac{v_0+v_1}{2}\right).
\end{equation}
Thus the branch anomaly $v_1-v_0$ is killed, total branch mass is preserved, and
fixed points are exactly branch-balanced vectors.

\begin{remark}
This is a genuine finite projection theorem.  It is not a theorem that arbitrary
trained transformer attention is Cuntz-exact, that semantic hallucination is
impossible, or that repeated LLM inference is thermodynamically lossless.
\end{remark}
