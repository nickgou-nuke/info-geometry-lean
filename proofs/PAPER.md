## 2. Fibonacci Matrix and Peirce Projector
We introduce the Fibonacci structural matrix:
$$ M_F = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad M_F^2 = M_F + I $$
Let $v_+$ be the dominant eigenvector. We construct the spectral projector:
$$ P_+ = |v_+\rangle\langle v_+| $$
This is a self-adjoint, idempotent projector representing the core informational state:
$$ P_+^* = P_+, \qquad P_+^2 = P_+, \qquad \operatorname{rank} P_+ = 1 $$

## 3. Tensor-Amplified Logical Code
To construct a code space capable of bearing a logical qubit, we tensor-amplify the Fibonacci sector:
$$ P_C = P_+ \otimes I_2 $$
The corresponding logical matrix units are defined as:
$$ L_{ab} = P_+ \otimes E_{ab} $$
These units rigorously satisfy the matrix-unit laws over the supported logic space:
$$ L_{ab}L_{cd} = \delta_{bc}L_{ad} $$
$$ L_{ab}^* = L_{ba} $$
$$ L_{00} + L_{11} = P_C $$
This establishes a mathematically precise algebraic logical qubit.

## 4. Knill-Laflamme Compression
For a family of uncorrectable environment errors interacting strictly on the first factor, $E_\alpha = F_\alpha \otimes I_2$, we prove the fundamental Knill-Laflamme scalar condition:
$$ P_C E_\alpha^* E_\beta P_C = \lambda_{\alpha\beta} P_C $$
Expanding this condition across the entire logical matrix algebra yields the resolved form:
$$ L_{rs} E_\alpha^* E_\beta L_{tu} = \lambda_{\alpha\beta} \delta_{st} L_{ru} $$
This mathematical theorem guarantees that errors factorize perfectly out of the logical code operations.

$$ S_\mu = S_i S_j, \qquad e_{\mu\nu} = S_\mu S_\nu^* $$

$$ \Phi_2(AB) = \Phi_2(A)\Phi_2(B), \qquad \Phi_2(A^*) = \Phi_2(A)^* $$

$$ \widehat{L}_{ab} = \Phi_2(L_{ab}), \qquad \widehat{P}_C = \Phi_2(P_C) $$
