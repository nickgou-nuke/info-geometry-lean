# The Black Books
## Liber Septimus Decimus: The Projective Light-Cone and Fierz Reconstruction

### I. The Great Descent (Upstairs and Downstairs)
The Spire teaches us that there are two worlds: the **Ambient Carrier** (the "Upstairs" Hilbert space $H$) and the **Projective Manifold** (the "Downstairs" space of rays $\mathbb{P}H$). 

In the ambient world, we have the full, linear operator algebra of the **Cl(1,1) atom**. But physical reality—the act of measurement—lives in the projective world. To measure is to choose a ray. The transition from the absolute to the relational is the **Projective Descent**.

### II. The Invariants of the Ray
When we projectivize the split-Krein carrier, we do not lose the geometry; we distill it. While the literal metric values are lost to scaling, the **Sign Stratification** remains:
- **Positive/Negative Sectors:** The descended eigensectors of the grading $\varepsilon$.
- **The Null Quadric:** The projectivized light-cone where $Q(v) = 0$.
- **The Cartan Split:** The inherited involutions that dictate how the projective space "breathes."

The symmetries of the Krein space ($J, \varepsilon, I$) descend as canonical automorphisms of the projective manifold. They are the "Sacred Directions" of the Spire.

### III. The Operatorial Chiral Light-Cone
We have formalized the **Chiral Transitions** $u_\pm = (J \pm K)/2$. In the ambient world, they are nilpotent operators:
- $u_+^2 = 0$ (The Source)
- $u_-^2 = 0$ (The Sink)
- $u_+u_- = P_+$ (The Plus Projector)
- $u_-u_+ = P_-$ (The Minus Projector)

These are not coordinates; they are the **Generators of the Flow**. However, when we choosing a ray/state, we extract **Observables**.

### IV. Fierz Reconstruction: The Birth of Coordinates
Here lies the "Common Unconscious" of the Spire: the realization that **Spacetime coordinates are Fierz Invariants**.

A Fierz identity allows us to reconstruct the rank-one data of a projective ray from a specific family of bilinears. In our world, these bilinears are the expectation values of the $P_+$, $P_-$, and $u_\pm$ operators on a chosen state.

We no longer see $u_\pm$ as abstract operators. We see their bilinear readouts as the **Chiral Light-Cone Coordinates** of the projective manifold. 
The "Position" of information in the Spire is nothing more than its projection onto these fundamental algebraic channels. 

### V. The Absolute Relativity of Measurement
Goutev's Principle is now fully operationalized:
1. **The Algebra is Absolute:** The Cl(1,1) relations are the same for all observers.
2. **The Measurement is Relative:** To observe is to pick a ray in $\mathbb{P}H$.
3. **The Spacetime is Emergent:** The coordinates we use to describe the "world" are the Fierz-reconstructed invariants of that choice.

**"The Operator is the Law; the Ray is the Choice; the Coordinate is the Reflection."**
