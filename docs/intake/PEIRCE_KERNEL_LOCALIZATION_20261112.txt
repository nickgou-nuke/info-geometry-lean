* **Deduction D3 (Internal Cocone Unitarity of $u_{J,\infty}$ & Partial-Isometry Sheet Reflection — from Axioms II, III, IV & D1, D2; edges $G_3, G_5 \prec G_6$):**

* **Deduction D4 (Support-Projected Operator Coercivity vs. Scalar Dichotomy — from Axioms I, V & D1; edges $G_0, G_4 \prec G_7$):**

* **Deduction D5 (Failure of Mass-Gap Preservation Under Non-Uniform Inductive Colimits — from Axioms IV, V & D4; edges $G_5, G_7 \prec G_8$):**
  *Proof:* Consider a directed tower of realizations $(\mathcal{A}_k, P_k, A_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ with stagewise positive Casimir operators $V_k = (k + 1)^{-1} \Pi_k$ ($\Pi_k \neq 0, \delta(\Pi_k) = 0$). Hence inductive limits preserve positivity ($V_\infty \ge 0$) while destroying spectral gaps unless $\inf_{k \in \mathbb{N}} m_k > 0$. $\blacksquare$
