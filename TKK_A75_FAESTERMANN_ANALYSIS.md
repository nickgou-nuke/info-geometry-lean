## Abstract

**Neutron deficient nuclei near ¹⁰⁰Sn have been produced by fragmentation of a 1 A GeV ¹¹²Sn beam.** The fragments were separated, identified and stopped in a highly segmented silicon strip detector stack. This detector measured the total energy of emitted β⁺-particles. γ-radiation was measured with surrounding detectors.

**Key findings:**

1. **Heaviest odd-odd nuclei (⁹⁰Rh, ⁹⁴Ag, ⁹⁸In):** First observation of fast β-decays compatible with **superallowed Fermi transitions**
2. **Confirmed superallowed decays:** ⁷⁸Y, ⁸²Nb, ⁸⁶Tc
3. **Long-lived T=0 states** observed in some nuclei
4. **rp-process waiting points:** Half-lives measured for **⁸⁰Zr to ⁹²,⁹³Pd**
5. **Proton drip line:** ⁷⁷Y decays dominantly via β-decay
6. **¹⁰²Sn Gamow-Teller strength:** **BGT = 4.0 ± 0.6** (one of largest known!)

## Key Physics Topics Covered

### 1. Superallowed Fermi β-Decays

**Nuclei studied:** ⁷⁸Y, ⁸²Nb, ⁸⁶Tc, ⁹⁰Rh, ⁹⁴Ag, ⁹⁸In

**Characteristics:**
- Fast β-decays
- 0⁺ → 0⁺ transitions
- Test of Conserved Vector Current (CVC) hypothesis
- Fundamental weak interaction physics

**TKK connection:** Superallowed decays are pure Fermi transitions with no Gamow-Teller component. Our TKK framework focuses on mixed Fermi+GT transitions in mirror nuclei. These are complementary probes of isospin structure.

### 2. Gamow-Teller Strength near ¹⁰⁰Sn

**Key result:** ¹⁰²Sn → BGT = 4.0 ± 0.6 (one of largest known!)

**Why this matters:**
- ¹⁰⁰Sn is doubly magic (Z=50, N=50)
- ¹⁰²Sn decay is a pure GT transition probe
- Large BGT indicates strong isospin mixing

**TKK comparison:** Our B(E1) asymmetry predictions test similar GT physics in mirror nuclei. The TKK formalism should be able to describe these large GT strengths via the triality-induced isospin mixing.

### 3. rp-Process Waiting Points

**Nuclei measured:** ⁸⁰Zr, ⁸⁴Mo, ⁸⁸Ru, ⁹²Pd, ⁹³Pd

**Significance:**
- These are β-decay bottlenecks in rapid proton capture
- Half-lives determine rp-process timescale
- Affects nucleosynthesis in X-ray bursts
- Solar abundances of ⁹²,⁹⁴Mo, ⁹⁶,⁹⁸Ru

**TKK opportunity:** Our framework could predict half-lives for rp-process waiting points using the TKK-ExtremeISB formalism with deformation and continuum effects.

### 4. Proton Drip Line

**Observation:** ⁷⁷Y decays dominantly via β-decay (not proton emission!)

**TKK relevance:** This is **weak binding physics** – exactly what matters for our ground-state inversion hypothesis! The drip line is where S_p → 0, and our A=67,71,75 candidates approach this regime.
