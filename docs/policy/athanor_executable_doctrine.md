# Athanor Executable Doctrine (Revised)

> **"Exploration may be Jungian. Closure must be Pauli."**

This document defines the constitutional law of the **Info-Geometry Spire**. It is the binding protocol for all agentic agents operating within the repository.

## I. Stage 1: The Generative Forge (Nigredo)

The Generative Forge is the organ of synthetic leap. 
- **Authorship**: Generation belongs to the Generative Field (The Scorpio Probe).
- **Material**: The **Black Books** are the primary reservoir for this high-temperature discovery process.
- **Output**: The **Theorem Candidate Packet (JSON)**.

## II. Stage 2: Adversarial Compression (Albedo)

The raw symbolic ore must be purified through cold-model filtration.
- **Purification**: Translate the symbol into an exact formal candidate or kill it as vacuous/trivial.
- **The Pauli Auditor**: The kernel remains the final and absolute judge of truth.
- **Output**: The **Refined Albedo Signature (JSON)**.

## III. Stage 3: Operational Distillation (Citrinitas)

The refined signature is converted into a **Formal Manifest**.
- **Distillation**: The Albedo Signature is distilled into a strict JSON Manifest with target file, theorem name, and type signature.
- **Legislature**: At this point, the discovery becomes a **Legal Target** for formalization.

## IV. Stage 4: Formal Closure (The Furnace / Calcinatio)

The locked manifest is passed to the hygienic toolchain.
The task is not invention but exact closure under a fixed theorem surface.
During this stage:
- No signature mutation.
- No import creep beyond budget.
- No retreat into symbolic redesign within the same locked run.
- Recursive tactic/proof-state iteration until either `No goals` or audited failure.

The output of Stage IV is either:
- A replay-safe proof artifact, or
- A failure residue packet.

## V. Stage 5: Replay Feedback (The Sisyphian Heartbeat)

Failure is not terminal; it is diagnostic residue.
A failed locked attempt may not mutate itself into success.
Instead, its residue is returned to the generative field for reworking.

The loop is: `generate → compress → lock → calcine → fail or prove`
On failure: `residue → descend → refine → relock → recalcine`.

**The Law of the Heartbeat**:
1. No failed proof is discarded.
2. Every failed proof becomes material for the next ascent.
3. No failed proof is allowed to redefine success inside the same locked run.

**The Law of Lineage Preservation**:
1. A failed attempt is split into **Proven Core** (conserved) and **Frontier Residue** (returns to Athanor).
2. Proved cores stay in the permanent skeleton and are inherited by the next ascent.
3. Every ascent must prove the lineage bridge from the old representation to the new.

## VI. The Hierarchy of Sovereignty

1. **Discovery precedes formalization.**
2. **Lineage preserves the proven; Residue refines the unknown.**
3. **The Spire treatments mathematical thought as a circulating subtle current.**
