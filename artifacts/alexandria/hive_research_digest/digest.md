# Hive arXiv research digest

Focused intake for the infinite/operatorial Souriau–Fisher–Onsager–metriplectic corridor.

## 1. Souriau-Fisher metric and Completely integrable system on Lie groups SO(2) and SO(3) (2509.20910)

Studies generalized Fisher metric on SO(2) and SO(3) via Souriau Lie-group thermodynamics; analyzes how 2-cocycles affect gradient-system integrability and how cocycles locally modify the Souriau-Fisher metric on coadjoint orbits.

## 2. Metriplectic formulations of variational thermodynamics (2410.11558)

Gives a metriplectic reformulation of variational non-equilibrium thermodynamics where solutions are generated by a Poisson bracket plus a metriplectic 4-bracket with Hamiltonian and entropy as generators; includes simple, discrete, Euler-Poincare and infinite-dimensional continuum cases.

## 3. Geometric thermodynamics for the Fokker-Planck equation: Stochastic thermodynamic links between information geometry and optimal transport (2209.00527)

Builds a geometric non-equilibrium thermodynamics picture linking entropy production to information geometry and optimal transport for the Fokker-Planck equation; relevant as external context for geometric entropy-production narratives, though not a repo-native owner theorem source.
