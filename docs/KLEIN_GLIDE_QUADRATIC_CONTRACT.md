## Dependency and causal order

1. The unit inversion relation yields transport across the inverse,
   commutation of the even square, and symmetric/antisymmetric identities.
2. Native polynomial evaluation separates central formal variables from
   elements obeying the inversion relation. The ordered residuals expose the
   missing commutation hypotheses.
3. The central trace and glide square give the multiplication relations of
   the native quaternion algebra and hence its universal algebra map.
4. The chosen scalar root `u` gives the matrix map of that same carrier. An
   explicit inverse proves splitting when `u-u⁻¹ ≠ 0`; an explicit kernel
   element proves failure of injectivity when `u=u⁻¹`.
5. Nilpotence/tripotence and CAR results constrain possible quotients. A
   separate full-matrix dual-number model disproves unconditional collapse.

These are dependencies of typed algebraic statements. No psychological or
physical causal mechanism is inferred from the narrative.

## Corrections and unproved adjacent claims

- Under the Klein relation and invertible glide, commutation means `u²=1`.
  It does not force an arbitrary operator to be a scalar sign.
- `G²` and `U+U⁻¹` commute with the generated algebra. The relation alone
  does not make them central in every larger ambient algebra.
- An ordinary central variable and an inversion-skew variable have different
  factorizations. In the skew case,
  `(x-u)(x+u⁻¹)=x²-1`, whereas
  `(x-u)(x-u⁻¹)=x²-2*u*x+1`.
- A nonzero nilpotent cannot satisfy `x³=x`. The polynomial
  `x(x-1)(x+1)=x³-x` remains valid in every ring.
- Quaternion conjugation fixes the coefficient ring. It is not the
  C*-adjoint of Klein group generators; no generic unitarity is claimed.
- A matrix equivalence after choosing a splitting root does not identify the
  unspecialized group algebra or its generic central localization with a
  full matrix ring. The branch representation is not faithful.
- Killing a CAR factor collapses a unital target because `ab+ba=1`.
  Nilpotence alone does not imply this.
- The full algebraic group-ring presentation, its exact center and domain
  property, the generic division/Brauer class, Azumaya locus, C*-completion
  and fibers, spinor descent, topological double cover, and general conjugacy
  classification are not acceptance claims of this change. Any literature
  discussion must be marked separately from Lean results.
