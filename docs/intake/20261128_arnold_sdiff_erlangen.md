## 2. The Five Structural Maps Connecting Flow Symmetries, Arnold's SDiff, and Klein Geometry

### Map 1 — Pushforward & Flow Conjugation = Klein's §4 Abbildung Transfer

Let `G = Diff(E)` (`smoothDiffeomorphismSubgroup E`). `G` acts naturally on two
dynamical carriers:

- Complete 1-parameter flows `Φ : Multiplicative ℝ →* G` by conjugation:
  `(φ · Φ)(t) := φ Φ(t) φ⁻¹`.
- Smooth vector fields `X : E → E` by Fréchet-derivative pushforward:
  `(φ_*X)(y) := Dφ(φ⁻¹(y)) (X(φ⁻¹(y)))`.

Differentiating `(φ ∘ Φₜ ∘ φ⁻¹)(y)` at `t = 0` intertwines the two actions:
`d/dt|₀ (φ · Φ)ₜ = φ_*X`.

Klein connection: Flow conjugation / vector-field pushforward is Klein's §4
Abbildung transfer (`klein_sec4_abbildung_perm_transfer`): two vector fields
`X, Y` represent the same dynamical geometry under `H ≤ G` iff they lie in the
same `H`-orbit, and their symmetry groups are conjugate:

`Sym_G(φ_*X) = φ Sym_G(X) φ⁻¹`.

### Map 2 — Lifting SDiff to the Erlangen Symmetry-Closure Operator

Given any family of invariants `ℐ` (measures `μ`, forms `α, ω`, metrics `g`,
Hamiltonians/Casimirs, or vector fields `ℱ`) and any subgroup `H ≤ G`:

- `Stab_G(ℐ) := {φ ∈ G | ∀ x ∈ ℐ, φ · x = x}`.
- `Inv(H) := {x | ∀ φ ∈ H, φ · x = x}`.

The Galois connection gives a closure operator on subgroups of `Diff(E)`:

`Cl_Erl(H) := Stab_G(Inv(H)) ≥ H`.

This is how Arnold's SDiff emerges as a symmetry closure:

- Start with the subgroup `H_(div=0)` generated by flows of divergence-free
  vector fields (e.g., Beltrami flows `smoothFlowHom s`).
- Their common geometric invariant is the reference measure `μ` (volume),
  i.e., `μ ∈ Inv(H_(div=0))`.
- Taking the Erlangen stabilizer of `{μ}` lifts the generated flow subgroup
  to its full symmetry closure:
  `Stab_Diff(E)({μ}) = SDiff(E, μ)`.

For an abstract family of vector fields `ℱ`, there are two distinct symmetry
lifts:

1. Pointwise symmetry (centralizer):
   `Sym_G(ℱ) = ⋂_(X∈ℱ) Stab_G(X)` (on flows, `φ Φₜ^X = Φₜ^X φ`).
2. Distribution / foliation symmetry (normalizer):
   `Norm_G(ℱ) = {φ ∈ G | φ_*(span ℱ) = span ℱ}` (allows
   time-reparametrization and mixing among generators — this is the
   Lie-symmetry group of an ODE/PDE system!).

### Map 3 — Klein's Subordination Lattice = Adjoining Flow Invariants

Adjoining geometric invariants reverses inclusion (`fixingSubgroup_antitone`,

`Diff(E) ≥ SDiff(E, μ) ≥ Cont(E, α) / Symp(E, ω) ≥ Isom(E, g) ≥ Sym_G(Φ^X)`.

The displayed labels are differential topology; Arnold ideal fluids
(preserves `μ`, `div X = 0`); Beltrami / Hamiltonian (preserves `α` or `dα`,
helicity); Riemannian / Killing (finite-dimensional Klein); flow centralizer.

### Map 5 — Infinitesimal Obstruction & Higher-Grade Emergence

At the Lie-algebra level, `φ_*X` differentiates to `ℒ_Y X = [Y, X]`:

- Infinitesimal symmetries of `X`: `sym(X) = ker(ad_X)`.
