To prevent the accidental reintroduction of absolute, standalone magnitudes at the Krein layer, two strict compiler-enforced conditions must be met for all L1 $\to$ L3 lifts:

### 1. The Descent Condition
All operator lifts onto the Krein/Doubled carrier must originate strictly from relative quantities (projective rays, relative log-densities, relative modular operators). A lift must never depend on a specific, absolute normalized representative of a ray. Normalization is a gauge choice; the lift must be gauge-invariant.

### 2. The Readout Condition
Any scalar physical quantity extracted on the Krein side (e.g., energies, entropies, metric readouts) must factor back through either:
- A relative/projective invariant.
- A transport-invariant operator identity (such as a commutator, Lie derivative, or established anomaly trace).
Scalars extracted from the Krein layer remain strictly readouts of relational dynamics, never independent physical primitives.
