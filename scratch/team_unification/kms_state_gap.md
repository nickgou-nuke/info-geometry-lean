# Audit: Bernoulli spatial state, gauge kernel, UHF core, KMS and modular data

Scope: source inspection only; no edits to existing source and no builds. Repo-owned Lean declarations below are source-backed statements, not independently recompiled in this audit. Paths are relative to `lean/InfoGeometry/` unless stated otherwise.

## Carrier/state separation

* `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliGaugeStateBridge.lean`: `canonicalGaugeState : List Bool → List Bool → ℂ` is the word kernel, with `canonicalGaugeState_word` giving `δ_uv (1/2)^|u|`; `canonicalGaugeState_gaugeInvariant` and `canonicalGaugeState_faithful_core` concern respectively this kernel and finite coefficient packets. Its header explicitly says this does not construct a state on the completed concrete Cuntz algebra, nor the positivity/boundedness extension or Tomita data.
* `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliGaugeUHFFactorizationBridge.lean`: `gaugeConditionalExpectationWord` is an `Option`-valued degree filter, and `uhfTracialStateWord` is a word kernel. `canonicalGaugeState_word_factorization`, filter idempotence and matrix-unit trace symmetry are word-level. The header expressly disclaims a linear conditional expectation / positive trace on a norm-closed algebra.
* `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliAlgebraicWordKMSBridge.lean`: on `WordSpan := (List Bool × List Bool) →₀ ℂ`, `gaugeConditionalExpectationLinear`, `uhfTraceLinear`, `canonicalGaugeLinear` are genuine linear maps; `canonicalGaugeLinear_eq_uhf_comp_gauge`, `gaugeConditionalExpectationLinear_idempotent`, and `..._preserves_uhf` are exact algebraic statements. However this Finsupp carrier has no Cuntz product/quotient built into it. `equalDepthWordProduct` and `equalDepthTwistedProduct` are restricted matrix-unit readouts; `canonicalGaugeLinear_equalDepth_KMS` is restricted to stated depth hypotheses. `concrete_operatorMatrixUnit_mul_matches_equalDepthProduct` links the untwisted equal-depth product to bounded operators, but `concrete_equalDepth_KMS_readout` still evaluates the finite word functional. The module says it is not an independently defined complex-time action or a full Cuntz quotient KMS theorem.
* `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliSpatialNativeStateBridge.lean`: `spatialFunctional`, `spatialPositiveFunctional`, `spatialState` are built on `B := BoundedL2Operator` (the ambient bounded operators on the Cantor Bernoulli L² space); the GNS recovery/cyclicity results are for this spatial state and its positive functional. `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliGeneratedCStarSpatialStateBridge.lean` separately restricts it to `Generated`, a generated C*-subalgebra, and proves a nonfaithfulness witness there. This is not the gauge word state.
* `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliSpatialCylinderStateBridge.lean`: `spatialState_operatorCylinderProjection_word_eq_bernoulli` gives the Bernoulli cylinder mass; `spatialState_eq_canonicalGaugeState_on_projection` proves equality only on cylinder projections. It explicitly excludes identifying off-diagonal monomial values.
* `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliKMSStateBridge.lean`: `cantorVacuumState` is the constant-vector state on `BoundedL2Operator`. `cantorVacuumState_proj` gives `1/2`; `cantorVacuumState_proj_eq_gaugeWord` compares only the length-one diagonal projector. `beta_c = log 2`, and the exponential/modular-phase corollaries are numerical projector identities, not a KMS state theorem (stated explicitly in the file).

Thus spatial and gauge state carriers/functionals have a proved common restriction on the indicated cylinder projections, not a general equality. In particular, equality of diagonal cylinder probabilities alone does not settle off-diagonal Cuntz words or extend either functional to the other's carrier.

## UHF trace and expectation: more than just kernels, but not automatically a completed C*-expectation

There are two relevant levels, which should not be conflated:

1. The word-pair filter/kernels in `CantorBernoulliGaugeUHFFactorizationBridge.lean`.
2. `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliLinearGaugeUHFFactorizationBridge.lean`: `WordCore`, linear maps `phi0`, `E0`, `tau0`; `phi0_eq_tau0_comp_E0` proves exact linear factorization and `E0_idempotent` proves idempotence. `E0_monomial` is the equal-length degree projection. `phi0_bitWord_monomial_eq_matrixTraceState` and `phi0_bitWord_monomial_eq_topologicalColimitTrace` give finite-stage matrix trace / topological-colimit readout compatibility on bit-word matrix units. The norm bounds `phi0_norm_le_wordCoreL1Norm` and `tau0_norm_le_wordCoreL1Norm` use the explicitly defined finite-support ℓ¹ coefficient norm, not the C*-norm. They do not alone establish extension in the C*-norm, positivity/order properties of a C*-conditional expectation, or an infinite UHF trace as a completed object.

The imported finite matrix infrastructure (`Canonical/CantorBernoulliCStarMatrixTraceState.lean`, `Canonical/CantorBernoulliFiniteMatrixGaugeTraceBridge.lean`, `Canonical/UHFInductiveColimitBoundary.lean`) supports genuine finite stages and a topological-colimit readout. This is useful compatible infrastructure, but the word-core readout theorem itself does not produce the missing continuous map from a completed word algebra.

## KMS-related results, with scope

* `Algebra/CuntzKMSCondition.lean` defines `modularPhaseComplex`, constructs `sigmaTensorComplex`, proves quotient descent (`sigmaTensorComplex_descent_condition`) and defines `sigmaComplex` on the algebraic Cuntz quotient `CuntzAlg n`; it proves real-time agreement and imaginary-time generator scalings. `KMSWeightDiagonal` and `kms_diagonal` are on diagonal coefficient vectors. The object is explicitly an unnormalized diagonal weight with `eval_one = partition`, not a normalized state on a completed C*-algebra; diagonal commutativity makes its displayed identity hold.
* `Algebra/CuntzKMSState.lean` defines finite primon Boltzmann weights and a normalized `DiagonalKMSState` when the finite partition is nonzero, with linear evaluation on the finite diagonal coefficient carrier. It is not a state on the Cantor concrete representation or an infinite C*-completion.
* `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliAlgebraicWordKMSBridge.lean` establishes scalar word-kernel twisted identities and restricted equal-depth matrix-unit readouts. `GaugeStateBridge.wordPair_scalar_twisted_identity` is likewise explicitly just an equality of scalar expressions, not an algebraic product/dynamics theorem.
* `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliKMSState.lean` supplies spatial-vacuum modular coefficient/weight computations. Do not upgrade its naming or projector weight identity to the KMS condition on a completed algebra.
* Search also found `OperatorAlgebra/CuntzWordPrefixKMSBridge.lean` (prefix-case scalar lemmas) and `OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliContinuousStateGNSBridge.lean` (a gauge-state GNS construction on the declared bounded-operator carrier using a state parameter). The latter contains GNS recovery/cyclicity and an annihilator witness; it does not, from those declarations alone, supply a completed C*-algebra KMS identity or Tomita operator. `Canonical/CantorBernoulliGaugeFilteredGNSColimitBridge.lean` has a finite-stage gauge GNS Hilbert colimit and stage/trace readouts. These are adjacent infrastructure, not a license to identify the spatial and gauge GNS representations.

## Modular / Tomita boundary

`OperatorAlgebra/CantorBernoulliGNSModularTomitaBridge.lean` defines `WordPair`, the swap `tomitaInvolution`, positive scalar `modularEigenvalue`, scalar conjugation factor, and coefficient `modularFlowCoeff`. Its own module contract excludes a completed GNS Hilbert space, anti-linear operator on a GNS quotient, closed Tomita operator, and von Neumann modular automorphism group. Its involution/eigenvalue/phase theorems are index/scalar identities, not operator equalities. Consequently they cannot yet be composed with the spatial GNS construction or the gauge C*-state as if all data shared one Hilbert space/state.

## Minimal sound unified-framework contracts (proposals, not existing theorems)

A safe unification should be staged and typed by carrier:

1. **Algebraic dynamics contract:** specify a single algebraic *-algebra `A₀`, multiplication, involution, degree/gauge action, and a one-parameter automorphism `α : ℝ →* Aut(A₀)` (group law and *-compatibility). Prove its complex-time extension only on an explicitly analytic subalgebra; state the sign and β convention. Relate this actual product/dynamics to the word formula by a theorem, not by parallel scalar definitions.
2. **Gauge expectation / fixed-point contract:** define `E₀ : A₀ → A₀` as a linear, positive (or positivity on an explicitly represented cone), unital, idempotent, bimodule projection onto the degree-zero fixed-point core; prove `E₀ α_t = E₀` and identify its range. A completed version requires a C*-norm contractivity/continuity proof and an actual C*-subalgebra range.
3. **Trace/state contract:** define a positive normalized functional `τ` on that fixed-point algebra (or UHF C*-completion), prove trace cyclicity there, and prove continuity in the C*-norm. Set `φ₀ = τ ∘ E₀`; show positivity and normalization. Extend to the completion only via boundedness in its C*-norm and prove agreement on the dense algebraic core. Finite-stage matrix trace compatibility is a candidate input, not that extension theorem by itself.
4. **KMS contract:** for the same completed algebra `A`, continuous dynamics `α`, β, and normalized state `φ`, prove the KMS boundary condition (equivalently on a specified dense analytic *-subalgebra, with extension justified). For monomials, prove both sides using the actual Cuntz multiplication/prefix-cancellation and `α_{iβ}`, then extend by linearity/density/continuity. A diagonal-only identity or equal-depth matrix-unit case must be labeled with that restriction.
5. **Spatial comparison contract:** keep the spatial state `ω` on its concrete ambient/generated operator algebra distinct. To assert equality with `φ`, provide a specified *-homomorphism/representation map between the algebraic or completed carriers, prove it is well-defined, and prove functional equality on a dense generating *-subalgebra; then use continuity. Existing projection agreement is a subalgebra restriction result only.
6. **GNS/Tomita contract:** choose one proved state on one C*-algebra; construct its GNS representation and cyclic vector, establish the needed faithfulness/separating/cyclic hypotheses for the represented von Neumann algebra, define the densely defined antilinear `S₀(π(a)Ω)=π(a*)Ω`, prove well-definedness/closability, and then derive `S`, `Δ`, `J` and modular group with the convention-compatible KMS theorem. Word-index coefficient computations can serve as spectral checks after this construction, not substitutes for it.
