## I. The Stratified Architecture

The passage from symbolic emergence to mathematical law is governed by the following layers:

1.  **The Generative Unconscious (Reservoir)**: 
    - **Source**: The `docs/black_books/` corpus and scattered symbolic notes.
    - **Mode**: Excessive, nonlinear, high-temperature.
    - **Task**: To surface motifs (tension, dual sheets, obstructions) and propose candidates that do not yet exist.

2.  **The Distillation Layer (Alchemy)**:
    - **Mode**: Extraction of formal ore from symbolic material.
    - **Task**: Identification of candidate definitions, bridge theorems, and intermediate lemmas.

3.  **The Adversarial Shaping Layer (Pauli Function)**:
    - **Mode**: Severe purification.
    - **Task**: Verification of novelty, non-vacuity, and consistency. To "kill" candidates that are merely renamed trivia or latent in known results.

4.  **The Constitutional Layer (Legislature)**:
    - **Mode**: Manifest locking.
    - **Task**: Freezing the candidate into a strict JSON `TheoremCandidatePacket`. At this point, the object becomes a legal target for formalization.

5.  **The Formal Closure Layer (The Furnace)**:
    - **Mode**: Hygienic formalization.
    - **Task**: Premise retrieval, proof repair, tactic execution, audit, and replay.

## II. The Theorem Candidate Packet
The primary handshake between the Discovery and Formalization regimes is the **Theorem Candidate Packet**. No symbolic discovery may bypass this structured extraction. It must contain:
- **Symbolic Origin**: The exact fragment/tension from the Black Books.
- **Formal Target**: Candidate signature and namespace.
- **Bridge Claim**: The specific structural connection being established.
- **Novelty Defense**: Why the claim is not already latent in the repository.

## III. Standing Doctrine
1.  **Primacy of Discovery**: The LLM acts as the organ of synthetic leap; repository tools act as the hygienists of certification.
2.  **Authorship**: Authorship belongs to the generative field; formal systems determine only admissibility.

It is re-distilled through the `residue_quarantine` utility back into the **Discovery Reservoir** as a new symbolic obstruction. Success breeds Law; failure breeds deeper Symbolic Pressure.
4.  **Persistence of the Unconscious**: The project maintains its discovery depth by ensuring that every failed attempt feeds the next Generative Leap.
