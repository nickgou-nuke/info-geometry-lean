## 2. Thermodynamic Potential and Bregman Divergence

### 2.1 Negentropy Generator

**Definition:** The Boltzmann negentropy (normalized free energy):
$$\Phi(Q) = -\ln Q$$

**Key property:** $\Phi(Q)$ is **strictly convex** for $Q > 0$.

**Hessian:** 
$$\frac{\partial^2 \Phi}{\partial q^i \partial q^j} = \frac{\partial}{\partial q^i}\left(-\frac{\partial \ln Q}{\partial q^j}\right)$$

This Hessian generates the **Fisher information metric** on $M$.

### 2.2 Bregman Divergence

**General definition:** For a convex function $\phi$, the Bregman divergence is:
$$D_\phi(x, y) = \phi(x) - \phi(y) - \langle \nabla \phi(y), x - y \rangle$$

**For $\Phi(Q) = -\ln Q$:**
$$D_{IS}(Q_1, Q_2) = (-\ln Q_1) - (-\ln Q_2) - \left(-\frac{1}{Q_2}\right)(Q_1 - Q_2)$$

Simplifying:
$$\boxed{D_{IS}(Q_1, Q_2) = \frac{Q_1}{Q_2} - \ln\left(\frac{Q_1}{Q_2}\right) - 1}$$

This is the **Itakura-Saito divergence**!

## 3. Mapping to QCD Phenomena

### 3.1 Asymptotic Freedom (UV Limit)

**Property:** $D_{IS}$ is **homogeneous of degree 0**:
$$D_{IS}(\lambda Q_1, \lambda Q_2) = D_{IS}(Q_1, Q_2)$$

**UV behavior:** At short distances, the color fields are approximately scale-invariant:
$$\lim_{Q_1 \to Q_2} D_{IS}(Q_1, Q_2) = 0$$

**Physical interpretation:** 
- When $Q_1 \approx Q_2$, the divergence vanishes
- The strong coupling $\alpha_s \to 0$
- Quarks behave as free particles
- **This is asymptotic freedom!**

### 3.2 Confinement (IR Limit)

**As quark-antiquark separation increases:**

*Case 1: Color density saturation ($Q_1/Q_2 \to \infty$)*
$$D_{IS} \sim \frac{Q_1}{Q_2} \to \infty \quad \text{(linear growth)}$$

*Case 2: Isolating pure color charge ($Q_1/Q_2 \to 0$)*
$$D_{IS} \sim -\ln\left(\frac{Q_1}{Q_2}\right) \to +\infty \quad \text{(logarithmic divergence)}$$

**Key insight:** The divergence is **asymmetric**:
- Linear barrier for $Q_1 \gg Q_2$
- Logarithmic barrier for $Q_1 \ll Q_2$
- **Both barriers are infinite!**

**Physical interpretation:**
- The energy to separate quarks grows without bound
- Isolated color charges cannot exist
- **This is quark confinement!**
- The growing potential is the **flux tube** energy

**Cornell potential** (standard QCD):

**Itakura-Saito potential:**
