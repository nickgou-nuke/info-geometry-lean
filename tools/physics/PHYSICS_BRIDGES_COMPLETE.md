## Critical Design Decisions

### 1. Split Signature Cl(n,n) NOT Euclidean

**Why**: Physics applications need indefinite metric:
- Krein spaces for thermal time
- 1+1 Minkowski for causal structure
- Bost-Connes uses C*-algebras with grading

**Not** Pauli matrices (Cl(3,0) or Cl(0,3)):
- Those have all generators squaring to same sign
- Wrong for relativistic / thermal physics

### 2. Theorem-Honest Bridges

GammaMatrices.lean is **NOT** a competing definition:
- Proves isomorphism to canonical Cl11CoordinateAlgebra
- No axioms, no fake proofs
- Pure constructive correspondence

### 3. Zorn Matrices Over Raw Octonions

**Why Zorn representation**:
- Makes SU(3) color decomposition manifest
- Clear triplet/antitriplet split from off-diagonals
- Easier to connect to gauge theory literature

Günaydin-Gürsey construction is the standard in physics.
