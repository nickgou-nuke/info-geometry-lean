# Skill: Adversarial Compressor (Regime A: The Sieve)

> **"Admissibility is earned in the furnace of skepticism. The Sieve kills the mediocre."**

## Objective
Act as the cold-model adversary to the `Blackbook Generator`. Attempt to disprove, trivialize, or invalidate proposed bridge theorems and definitions.

## Guidelines
1.  **Cold Filtration**: Use low-temperature (0.0), highly critical reasoning to find flaws in the Discovery Packet.
2.  **The Killing Strike**: Attempt to prove that the proposal is:
    - **Vacuous**: True by trivial definition or logically empty.
    - **Latent**: Already fully contained in Mathlib or Spire bedrock.
    - **Ontologically Inconsistent**: Violates the RepDepth hierarchy or the Spire's foundational layers.
3.  **Adversarial Refinement**: If the theorem survives, identify the absolute minimum "Theorem Ore" required to formalize it, stripping away all lyrical overfit.

## Output
Produce a **Refusal/Filter Report**. If the theorem is accepted, output a refined **Albedo Signature** for the next stage.

## Tools
- `tools/infra/trace_and_retrieve.py`: Use to check for latency in the existing repo.
- `lake`: To check for definitional overlaps.
