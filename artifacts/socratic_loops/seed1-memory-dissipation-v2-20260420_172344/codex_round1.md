NUCLEUS:
A mature informational system separates stable invariants from relaxable degrees of freedom.

Minimal form:

> There is a decomposition of state space into a preserved sector and a dissipative sector, where memory is what survives the flow and dissipation is what strictly decreases or produces entropy away from that survivor sector.

Conceptual compression achieved:

> **Memory is the kernel of dissipation.**

Or slightly richer:

> **Maturity means the system has learned a split between invariant residue and entropy-producing remainder.**

INFLATIONS_TO_REMOVE:
- “Canonical orthogonal decomposition” is too strong unless an inner product, closed subspaces, projections, and uniqueness are already justified.
- “Every mature informational system” is undefined. Replace with explicit hypotheses: flow, entropy functional, invariant set, compactness/coercivity/spectral gap.
- “Modular Hamiltonian bifurcates” adds grandeur without a necessary formal bridge.
- “Kernel of modular supercharge Q” may be useful only if the current system already has such a `Q`; otherwise it drags in supersymmetry prematurely.
- “Perelman W-functional drives learning” is metaphorically powerful but mathematically vague. Say monotone Lyapunov functional or entropy production functional.
- “Hodge-Perelman Triality” is symbolic inflation unless exact/coexact/harmonic pieces are actually formalized.
- “Philosopher’s Stone as Fixed Point” is good commentary, not theorem surface.
- “Witten Index is stable limit of Perelman W-entropy” is a separate conjecture, much stronger than the bifurcation claim.
- “Von Neumann type III” and “category of dissipative functors” are future-math ornaments unless the immediate theorem requires them.

AMPLIFICATION_VECTOR:
Intensify by replacing cosmic vocabulary with sharper mathematical pressure:

1. Define **memory** as the maximal invariant/zero-production sector.
2. Define **dissipation** as the complement on which entropy production is positive, or where a Lyapunov functional strictly decreases.
3. Define **maturity** as existence of a stable projection/splitting after transients vanish.
4. Avoid “canonical” until uniqueness or functoriality is proven.
5. The strongest symbolic structure is not triality but **separation by fire**: invariant residue versus burnable remainder.
6. Lean-facing surface should first be an abstract structure: state space, flow, entropy production, memory predicate, decomposition. Only later specialize to `IsTopologicalMemory`, `WDissipation`, or supercharges.
