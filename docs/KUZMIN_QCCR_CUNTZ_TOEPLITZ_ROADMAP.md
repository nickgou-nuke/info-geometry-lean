## Scope

Kuzmin proves that for real `q` with `|q| < 1`, the C*-algebra generated by
the Fock representation of the q-canonical commutation relations is
isomorphic to the Cuntz-Toeplitz algebra:

```text
B_{n,q} ~= B_{n,0} ~= KO_n.
```

The repository does not currently prove this C*-classification theorem.  The
repo-owned formal surface is a finite algebraic q-CCR layer, plus matrix
witnesses for small q-Fock blocks.

## Source Theorem Targets

The paper's proof route decomposes into theorem targets that can be
formalized only after the required C*-algebra infrastructure is available:

1. q-deformed Fock space and left/right creation-annihilation operators.
2. Compact-ideal invariance and quotient algebra `C_{n,q}`.
3. Approximately inner flip for the fixed-point quotient.
4. Asymptotic embedding into the UHF algebra `U_{n^infty}`.
5. Crossed-product presentation by an endomorphism.
6. Kirchberg-Phillips classification for the quotient.
7. K-theory computation via the Pimsner-Voiculescu sequence.
8. Gabe-Ruiz classification of the unital extension.
9. Final isomorphism `B_{n,q} ~= B_{n,0}` for `|q| < 1`.

## Stable Lemma Targets

QCCRGeneral.flip_square:

QCCRGeneral.T_norm_lt_one:

## Open Debt

- The repo does not prove faithfulness of the Fock representation for the full
  q-CCR universal C*-algebra.
- The repo does not define the full q-Fock Hilbert completion for arbitrary
  `n` and all tensor degrees.
- The repo does not prove the compact ideal, quotient, fixed-point algebra,
  or crossed-product presentation from Kuzmin.
- The repo does not own the required Kirchberg-Phillips or Gabe-Ruiz
  classification machinery.
- The repo does not prove the K-theory computation for `C_{n,q}`.
- SymPy/Sage/GAP witnesses are navigation evidence only; they cannot certify
  C*-classification theorems.
