`orbitWeylRepresentative`, and `concreteBNGeneratorSubgroup`. The accepted
`G2CorrectedBruhatCover` and `G2SignatureCellSeparation` establish coverage and
separation. `G2TitsRepresentativeInjectivity.orbitWeyl_bijective` converts the

form is a word in `s,t`, since `t=s*c` and `s*s=1`. Every covered element
is therefore in the subgroup generated by U,s,t. No BN2 premise or legacy
quotient injectivity is introduced. Individual cell cardinalities and the
simple-reflection two-cell transition remain separate obligations.

`G2TitsRepresentativeInjectivity`, `G2TwoConcreteWeylG2`, and the two accepted
