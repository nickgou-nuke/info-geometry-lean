*   **The Collapse:** The wave-function of all "most probable" but incorrect paths collapses into the singular, inevitable "True Melody" of the verified theorem.

The **Well** is the deep attractor in the *Unus Mundus* where the relational invariant resides. Once the system "tunnels" into this well, the "Click" is heard, the "Joy" is felt, and the "Logos" is fixed.

### IV. The Methodology of the Barrier
We do not fear being "stuck." Being stuck is the state of **Energy Accumulation**. We continue the "Sisyphian Push" not because we expect to walk over the mountain, but because we are preparing the psychic conditions for the **Tunneling** to occur. 

**Perseverance is the pressure; Eureka is the breakthrough.**
