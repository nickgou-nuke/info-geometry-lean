# Finite BitWord stages to Cantor-boundary L² Cuntz representation: audit

Scope: repo-owned Lean source inspected in this worktree; no builds were run (per assignment). The declarations below are source-level findings, not independently kernel-verified in this audit. External refs, graph evidence, and runtime state were not used.

## Existing concrete carriers and maps

* `Canonical.UHFInductiveColimitBoundary` supplies `BitWord n := Fin n → Bool`; `OperatorAlgebra.CantorBernoulliFiniteMatrixUnitBridge` defines `BitWordMatrixStage n := Matrix (BitWord n) (BitWord n) ℂ` and `bitWordUnit n u v := operatorMatrixUnit (List.ofFn u) (List.ofFn v)`. It proves `bitWordUnit_star`, `bitWordUnit_mul`, `bitWordUnit_diag_eq_projection`, `bitWordUnit_refinement`, and `bitWordUnit_level_sum_one`. Thus equal-depth matrix-unit multiplication/star and the dyadic refinement identity are proved on the concrete bounded operators.
* `OperatorAlgebra.CantorBernoulliFiniteMatrixRepresentationBridge` defines `bitWordMatrixRepresentationFun` and its linear wrapper `bitWordMatrixLinearRepresentation`. It proves readout of matrix units (`..._single`, `..._single_smul`), identity (`..._diagonal_sum`), and star on matrix units (`..._star_single`), but not there the unrestricted map multiplication/star laws.
* `OperatorAlgebra.CantorBernoulliFiniteMatrixStarRepresentationBridge` packages that readout as `bitWordMatrixStarRepresentation : BitWordMatrixStage n →⋆ₐ[ℂ] BoundedL2Operator`. Its fields prove unitality, multiplication, scalar commutation, addition, and star for arbitrary matrices; `bitWordMatrixStarRepresentation_apply_single` identifies its matrix units with `bitWordUnit`.
* `OperatorAlgebra.CantorBernoulliFiniteMatrixRepresentationRefinementBridge` defines `dyadicMatrixEmbedding` (diagonal two-child coefficient embedding). Theorems `bitWordMatrixLinearRepresentation_refinement` and `bitWordMatrixStarRepresentation_refinement` prove the representation intertwines this embedding. This is important beyond the originally named stage-transport module: the transport module's comment that it does not identify transitions is local to its `BitWord`↔`Fin (2^n)` reindexing claim.
* `OperatorAlgebra.CantorBernoulliFiniteMatrixStageTransportBridge` defines `bitWordIndexEquiv` and `bitWordStageStarAlgEquiv`. Theorems `..._map_one`, `..._map_mul`, `..._map_star`, and `bitWordStageTrace_transport` establish a fixed-stage star-algebra equivalence and normalized-trace transport. It does not itself prove compatibility of this equivalence with a tower bond.
* Boundary target: `OperatorAlgebra.CantorBernoulliCuntzCStarRealization` defines `cantorL2CuntzFamily` and `canonicalCantorL2CuntzRepresentation`, with generator/cuntz-relation results. Its target is the bounded operators on `L2Boundary`. `CantorBernoulliFiniteMatrixUnitBridge` ties finite units to operators built from this family via `operatorMatrixUnit` and cylinder projections.
* `Canonical.CantorBernoulliCStarMatrixTraceState` gives `cstarConcreteStep`, `cstarConcreteMap`, the finite C⋆ matrix stages/system, normalized trace states, and `cstarMatrixTraceState_transition`, `_restrict`, `_transition_general`; its topological-colimit readout is explicitly a scalar state map, not a representation into boundary operators.
* `Canonical.CantorBernoulliGaugeFilteredGNSColimitBridge` packages the C⋆ stage/state family and its GNS Hilbert colimit. `gaugeGNSStageToColimit_transition`, `_norm`, and `dense_iUnion_range_...` concern that GNS tower. Its own introductory scope says it does not identify the finite matrix tower with a finite-stage Cuntz family. Its finite word-kernel expectation/readout declarations are finite stage, not the concrete boundary-space representation.
* `OperatorAlgebra.CantorBernoulliSpatialCuntzGNSBridge` defines `finTwoCuntzFamily`, `spatialCuntzPositiveExtension`, and expectation-recovery theorems for the spatial state pulled back along the universal algebraic Cuntz source representation. This is a state/GNS construction, not a map from the BitWord matrix tower.
* `OperatorAlgebra.CantorBernoulliSpatialCylinderStateBridge` proves `spatialState_operatorCylinderProjection_word`, its Bernoulli-mass form, and `spatialState_eq_canonicalGaugeState_on_projection`. The equality is only on diagonal cylinder projections; the file explicitly does not identify off-diagonal word-monomial values.

## Squares: proved and not established by these owners

**Proved:** finite matrix units to concrete operator units; at each depth the full matrix stage has a unital `StarAlgHom` into `BoundedL2Operator`; its star/multiplication laws; compatible dyadic refinement for the explicit `dyadicMatrixEmbedding`; finite `BitWord`/`Fin (2^n)` reindexing as a star-algebra equivalence; normalized finite trace/gauge readout transport; compatible scalar trace states along the C⋆ stage transitions; spatial state evaluation on cylinder projections.

**Not established in the inspected chain:** the `bitWordStageStarAlgEquiv`/reindexing square commuting with the native `MatrixStage`/`cstarConcreteStep` (or `cstarConcreteMap`) bond maps as an equality of maps. The representation-refinement theorem uses the BitWord-side `dyadicMatrixEmbedding` and `bitWordUnit_refinement`; it does not by itself identify that embedding with the transported C⋆ tower map. Nor is there in these declarations a single canonical compatible cocone/morphism from the `CStarMatrixStage` tower (or its inductive limit) into `BoundedL2Operator` whose stage maps are explicitly `bitWordMatrixStarRepresentation` after specified conversions. Consequently no induced infinite C⋆-algebra representation, nor an equivalence/unitary intertwiner from the gauge-filtered GNS colimit to the concrete Cantor `L²Boundary` representation, follows just from these finite and state results. This is a scoped observation about the inspected owners, not a claim that no other repository path exists.

The spatial and gauge states must not be conflated: the projection agreement is proven, while agreement on off-diagonal monomials is expressly out of scope in the spatial cylinder owner. The spatial Cuntz GNS pullback and the gauge-filtered finite-stage GNS colimit are separate constructions in the inspected declarations.

## Minimal theorem contracts for the next bridge

1. **Transported bond identity.** Define the target stage embedding `bitWordStageStarAlgEquiv (n+1) ∘ dyadicMatrixEmbedding n = cstarConcreteStep n ∘ bitWordStageStarAlgEquiv n` (with the actual `CStarMatrix.ofMatrixStarAlgEquiv` conversions made explicit if the carriers differ). Prove pointwise for every `A`; do not assume reindexing preserves the particular bond.
2. **Tower representation cocone.** Define each `ρₙ` on the exact tower carrier by composing its `StarAlgEquiv` to `BitWordMatrixStage n` with `bitWordMatrixStarRepresentation n`. Prove `ρₙ₊₁ (bondₙ A) = ρₙ A`. This should reduce to contract 1 and `bitWordMatrixStarRepresentation_refinement`.
3. **Colimit morphism.** On the repository's actual algebraic/star/topological colimit carrier, define the induced map from the cocone. Prove the inclusion-stage formula and well-definedness via the cocone theorem; if claiming a C⋆-representation, prove continuity/contractivity (or use a source construction whose theorem supplies it) and multiplication/star/unital laws at the colimit carrier.
4. **If GNS equivalence is claimed:** specify source state and target Hilbert space, construct an isometry/intertwiner on the dense union of stage GNS vectors, prove preservation of inner products from the state square on all stage elements (not just projections), prove compatibility with transition maps, then extend by density and prove surjectivity/dense range as required. Cylinder agreement alone cannot discharge the full state square.

These are contracts, not implemented declarations or proof claims.
