## Interpretation
- `Min depth` is the shortest causal distance from the root set.
- `Max depth` is the deepest causal distance from the root set; this is the **theoretical age**.
- `Reverse load` measures how much later theory depends on the node/component.
- `Debt` is weighted from the tracked thinness/vacuity/surrogate queues.
- `Inertia = reverse load / (1 + debt)`.
- Vacuity and surrogate debt are penalized more heavily than thinness.
