# The Athanor Executable Doctrine

> **"Newton is the model for birth; Lean is the model for survival; the Spire is the engine of recursive transformation."**

This document codifies the five machine-implementable stages of mathematical purification within the Info-Geometry Spire. It transforms symbolic pressure into verified Law through a closed-loop alchemical process.

## I. Stage 1: Generative Wilderness (The Scorpio Probe)
The loop begins in the latent field. The agent acts as the **Scorpio Probe**, extracting "pre-rigorous" symbols from the **Black Books** reservoir.
- **Mode**: High-temperature, unconstrained discovery.
- **Priority**: Symbolic emergence, bridge-making, and motivic identification (e.g., dual sheets, tension).
- **Admissible Confession**: Poetic imagery and lyrical overfit are accepted at this stage as "discovery ore."

## II. Stage 2: Adversarial Compression (Albedo Purge)
The raw symbolic ore is passed through the **Pauli Auditor**.
- **Mode**: Low-temperature, highly critical filtration.
- **Task**: Destruction of lyrical overfit. Rejection of vacuous, trivial, or inconsistent candidates.
- **Output**: A crystalline **Albedo Signature** that maps discovery to an exact formal candidate.

## III. Stage 3: Manifest Lock (Legislative Freeze)
The Albedo Signature is frozen into a **Theorem Candidate Packet**.
- **Mode**: Canonical locking.
- **Constraint**: Name, type, namespace, and target file are manifest-locked. No signature mutation is permitted beyond this point.
- **Legislature**: At this point, the discovery becomes a **Legal Target** for formalization.

## IV. Stage 4: Formal Closure (The Furnace)
The locked manifest is passed to the **Hygienic Toolchain** (LeanDojo, LeanCopilot, LeanInteract).
- **Mode**: Goal-conditioned proof search and repair.
- **Execution**: Recursive tactic iteration until the Lean goal state is `No goals`.
- **Integrity**: Enforce the **Triple-Gate Audit** (Build, Audit, Replay).

## V. Stage 5: Replay Feedback (The Recursion)
Failure is not terminal; it is diagnostic residue.
- **The Loop**: Failed proofs that cannot be closed in Regime B are returned to the **Generative Wilderness** (Regime A).
- **Refinement**: The specific obstruction or compiler error is fed back to the Scorpio Probe as a new symbolic pressure point.
- **Transformation**: The system iterates until either the bridge is formalized or the symbolic path is discarded.
